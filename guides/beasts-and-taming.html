Beasts and taming

Taming

  1. Cook a Monster Treat (Cooking 5: one Corn and one Raw Fish at a stove or campfire), or buy one from

Ranch Master Tobin.

  1. Weaken a monster: the more damage it has taken, the better your chance.
  2. Stand next to it, right-click and choose Tame. A failed try still uses the treat.

Each beast has a Taming level (see the beast list). You can try from 20 levels below it. About one beast in 64 has a rare colour.

Companions

Your first beast follows you and attacks whatever you fight. Open the Beasts window (N) to choose who follows, rename beasts, or release them. You can carry six.

The ranch

Wrenford Ranch is south of Riverside Cottages. Next to Ranch Master Tobin you can send beasts to the pen. They make goods over time (cows give Milk, wolves Hide, yetis Wool, gryphons Feathers and Eggs...). Feed them Corn or a treat to double it for fifteen minutes. With Ranching 10, two beasts of the same kind, both level 5 or more, can raise young that inherit their parents' strengths.

Beast battles

Right-click a trainer and choose Beast battle, or challenge another player. Up to three beasts a side; each turn you pick a move or swap. Fire beats Wind, Wind beats Earth, Earth beats Water, Water beats Fire; Light and Shadow beat each other. Trainers: Sergeant Holt, Wren the Herbalist, Ranch Master Tobin and the three-round Castle Tournament with Sir Trianus.
