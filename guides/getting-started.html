Getting started

You wake in Dawnrest Hollow after washing up by the pond. Elder Maren is waiting.

Your first quests

  1. A New Dawn: talk to Elder Maren.
  2. Sword Practice: Sergeant Holt on the Dawnrest Training Green wants three Slimes cleared. You get a

Short Sword and Small Shield, and the gate to the Whisperwood opens.

  1. Frog Venom: help Wren the Herbalist in the Old Oak Glade.
  2. Welcome to Wrenford: meet Mayor Aldous in the market square.
