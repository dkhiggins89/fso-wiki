Getting started

You wake inside Elder Maren's Cottage in Dawnrest Hollow, and Elder Maren is waiting by the stove. Walk out of the door at the bottom of the room to start exploring.

Everyone starts the same way: an Adventurer in plain clothes, with a short sword and some bread, and every stat at 1. There is no class to pick. Your stats grow from the skills you use, and your two highest stats decide your class, so you become a Fighter, Mage, Ranger or something rarer just by how you play (see Classes and stats). Sergeant Holt's first quest adds a shield.

Fighting

Right click a monster and choose Attack: you walk up and keep swinging until it falls. The colour after its name tells you how the fight will go for you, from grey (Trivial) through green, yellow and orange to red (Deadly): start with green ones. Monsters also show their level: Wrenford Vale's are levels 1-5, and each region beyond has its own band, shown as you walk in (see Levels). Monsters in the wild notice you when you come within a few steps and come at you; anything you hit fights back. When a monster glows red and shows a ! it is winding up a heavy blow: face it and Guard, or step away and the blow misses. Walk away and it gives up after a short chase, going back to where it lives. Farm animals never bother you. If you are hit while standing still you fight back by yourself, and a few seconds after a fight you get your health back quickly.

Put food in a quick slot (click an empty one beside the ability bar) and press F5 to eat mid-fight. If you are knocked out, a window shows what hit you and how hard. The world map (M) shades every place by how it suits your level and names the regions that suit you; once you have touched a starstone it offers Return to that stone every 15 minutes.

Your first quests

  1. A New Dawn: talk to Elder Maren.
  2. Sword Practice: Sergeant Holt on the Dawnrest Training Green wants three Slimes cleared. You get a

Short Sword and Small Shield, and the gate to the Whisperwood opens.

  1. Frog Venom: help Wren the Herbalist in the Old Oak Glade.
  2. Welcome to Wrenford: meet Mayor Aldous in the market square.

After that, the quest log (Q) shows every quest you can take, by region and level, and the world map (M) marks the people offering them with a "!". Ill Omens at Millbrook starts the Vale Lands story. Some quests ask you to choose, and what you choose changes how they end and which quests you are offered later. Guards and fishers have a daily quest that comes back every day.

What next

  • Train gathering skills: Mining, Lumberjacking, Fishing.
  • Turn materials into gear: see Crafting chains.
  • Tame your first beast: see Beasts and taming.
  • Own a home: each big city has a housing district behind a gate from its market. The Land Agent there gives the

quest A Place of Your Own; its deed and enough gold buy a plot. Mind the weekly tax.
