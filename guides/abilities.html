Abilities

Press A for the Abilities window. There is no class to choose and no points to spend: each weapon family has three arts, and the skill that powers the family unlocks them. Train what you want to be good at.

ArtKeyUnlocks at the family's skill
IZ1 (you have it from the start)
IIShift+Z15
IIICtrl+Z35

Arts use Focus (50% for I and II, 75% for III), which refills over a minute. Swap weapons and the bar swaps: a swordsman who picks up a bow starts the bow's set at I and keeps his sword set.

  • Cleave hits the foe in front hard and clips the foes beside you. Riposte parries the next blow and strikes back.

Whirlwind hits every foe around you.

  • Puncture is two stabs. Evade slips the next blow. Backstab hits a foe facing away from you for two and a half

times the damage.

  • Stunning Blow stuns for 4 seconds. Armour Break lowers the monster's defence by 3. Earthshaker stuns the foe

and everything around it.

  • Volley (three arrows), Pinning Shot (pins for 3 seconds) and Barrage (six arrows).
  • Arcane Blast ignores defence. Mana Shield absorbs the next three blows. Chain Blast leaps to two more monsters.
  • Flurry is three fast blows. Disorient stuns for 5 seconds. Pressure Point hits three times as hard, ignoring defence.
