Regional atmosphere

Snowy outdoor maps in Skaldmark show a soft green and teal aurora at night. In Soleira, La Mancha and the Golden Hills have occasional small dust devils in daylight. They drift across clear ground and fade. In Eisenwald, the fir forest of the Eisenwald and the Beechwood fill with slow drifting mist at night and in the early morning; it lifts by midday and never comes with rain. These are cosmetic effects and do not affect combat, movement, crops or items.

Reduce Motion disables the dust devils and makes the aurora and the mist static and softer. Turning off HD graphics or lighting also hides these regional visuals. They stay out of interiors and caves.

Near water in Port Saltreach, Ravnsund, Isvik and Puerto Dorado, listen for quiet surf and occasional gull calls. The Troll Fells and Sierra Parda have their own wind. Settings → Ambience controls these sounds, including mute. The sound settings work independently of the visual effects.

The sounds are original synthesis, made without downloaded or recorded samples. Other maps keep their existing ambience. See Northern lights and dust devils.
