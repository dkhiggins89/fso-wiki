Regional instruments and lore

Instruments

Use a Lyre or Guitar from inventory to open Songs. Choose a song and repeat the shown phrase with the note buttons. The instrument stays in your backpack. Songs can still be opened from the skill menu without an instrument. The item pages show the current Carpentry recipes and requirements.

Reading

Use the Skaldmark Saga or Soleira Chronicle to open its reading window. Scroll if needed and close it with the window button or Escape. Books are reusable: reading consumes nothing, awards no experience and teaches no spell. Their full text is generated on each book's item page from the same lore used in the game.

Once the new regions arrive, Skald Eirik in Vindholm supplies the lyre and saga; Innkeeper Rafaela supplies a guitar and wine; Merchant Isidro has the chronicle.

Grapes and wine

Use Grape Seeds on prepared soil through Farming. Watering and tending use the existing crop controls. Let the crop ripen before harvesting Grapes; if the backpack is full, the ripe crop is kept until there is room.

Fruit Seller Carmen and Farmer Dolores stock the seeds in Soleira. Carmen also sells grapes. The item pages and Farming guide show requirements from the rule tables. A small vineyard gives Los Molinos a local crop beside its mills.

Make Soleira Wine with grapes and Cork at a Stove or Campfire through Cooking. The wine page shows its recipe. Grapes and wine use the ordinary food and drink rules, including the full-hunger limit.
