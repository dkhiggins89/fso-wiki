Making NPCs move

In the map editor (F10), right click a placed NPC and choose Edit NPC <name>.... The box This <name> at <map> x, y holds the settings for that one placement. The same NPC placed somewhere else keeps its own settings.

Faces is the way it looks: South, East, North or West. Next to it, choose what it does:

ChoiceWhat it doesSettings
Stands stillStays on its cell, facing the way chosen.None
Turns on the spotStays on its cell and turns now and then.Turns to: tick at least two directions. every: about how many seconds between turns (a little sooner or later each time).
Walks a set pathWalks to each waypoint in turn, finding its own way round walls.Draw path on the map, Go round (else it walks back the same way), wait at each point in seconds.
Wanders freelySteps about at random, pausing 2 to 6 seconds between steps.Wanders up to: how many cells it may stray from where it is placed.

Press Save NPC to keep the settings. A south-facing NPC that stands still is saved exactly as NPCs always were.

Drawing a path

  1. Choose Walks a set path and press Draw path on the map. The editor window steps aside.
  2. Click the cells the NPC should walk to, in order. Its own cell is the start, so do not click it. Each point gets a

number and a line from the one before.

  1. Right click or Backspace removes the last point. Enter finishes and Esc cancels.
  2. Press Save NPC.

Waypoints must be on the NPC's own map and on walkable ground. You only need the corners: the NPC finds its own way between points. With the editor open you can see the routes of the map's walking NPCs as faint blue lines, and how far each wanderer may go as a green circle.

Good to know

  • Walking NPCs wait rather than walk through players, monsters, objects or other NPCs.
  • Wanderers keep off the map edge (where players cross to the next map) and never step next to another NPC, so they

never stand in front of a shop counter.

  • The towns' NPCs were given states by role with tools/world/set_npc_states.py, which leaves alone any NPC that

already has a facing or a behaviour. Change any of them in the editor as you like.

  • Wanderers never stand on a door or map exit, or on the cell in front of one. A route goes through a door cell only

if you made that cell a waypoint.

  • Every NPC turns to face a player who talks to it and holds still while they talk, then carries on.
  • Quests, shops, banking, bounties and the other NPC services work wherever the NPC is standing. The player only has

to be next to it, as before.

  • The world check (validator) refuses a facing other than S, E, N or W, an unknown behaviour, and a path waypoint off

the map. It warns about a waypoint on ground nobody can walk on.

For the map file format (facing and move on an npcs entry), see game/core/npc_behaviour.gd.
