One-Eyed Menace

One-Eyed Menace
KindRegion
Level8
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartShepherd Aldric in The Ninestones
RequirementsWelcome to Wrenford
Hand inShepherd Aldric
Rewards80 gold

Cyclopes from the northern hills are taking sheep from the Ninestones.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Shepherd Aldric in The Ninestones. “The one-eyed brutes come down from the hills and take my sheep two at a time. Drive them back.”
  2. Defeat 4 Cyclopes in the northern hills: defeat 4 × Cyclops.
  3. Return to Shepherd Aldric. “Quiet nights at last. Thank you.”
