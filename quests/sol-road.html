Into the Sun

Into the Sun
StartBorder Warden Ines in Fenmire Crossing
RequirementsWelcome to Wrenford
Hand inBorder Warden Ines
Rewards50 gold
60 experience
3 × Oranges

Find Duchess Leonor in the Alcazaba of Castamar.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Border Warden Ines in Fenmire Crossing. “You came through the Fenmire? Then the Duchess will want to see you. Follow the road through the cork woods to Corcova, then south to Castamar on its hill.”
  2. Speak with Duchess Leonor in Castamar: talk to Duchess Leonor.
  3. Return to Border Warden Ines. “So she has a task for you already. That's the Duchess.”
