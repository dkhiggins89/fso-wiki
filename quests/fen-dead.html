The Drowned Dead

The Drowned Dead

Put 3 Zombies of the Fenmire to rest.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Mother Nettle in Nettle's Hut. “The old chapel in the fen sank long ago, and its dead don't care to stay down. Lay three of them to rest and I'll make it worth your while.”
  2. Defeat Zombies in the Fenmire: defeat 3 × Zombie.
  3. Return to Mother Nettle. “There. Quieter already. Take these; you'll want them in the fen.”
