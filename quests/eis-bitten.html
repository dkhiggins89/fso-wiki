The Bitten Brother

The Bitten Brother
KindStory
Level16
RegionEisenwald
StartHunter Florian in Tannheim Carvers' Lane
RequirementsThe Iron Wood
Hand inHunter Florian
RewardsCure: 180 gold, 1200 experience, 3 × Beast Tonic
End: 320 gold, 1200 experience

Florian's brother was bitten by a werewolf. Save him, or put him out of his misery.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Hunter Florian in Tannheim Carvers' Lane. “My brother Veit was bitten on the last full moon. He's changing. Sister Hildegard says there may be a cure, but I... I don't know. Help me.”

1. Hunt the werewolves that bit Veit.

  • Defeat 3 Werewolves: defeat 3 × Werewolf.

2. Ask Sister Hildegard at the Forest Chapel about a cure.

  • Ask Sister Hildegard about a cure: talk to Sister Hildegard.

“There is a blessing, if it is not too late: herbs steeped in the chapel's well water, given before the next moon. It may fail. Some would say it is kinder to end it quickly. Which will you try?”

A choice:

  • “We try the blessing. Veit deserves the chance.” leads to Gather herbs for the blessing and bring them to Sister Walburga.
  • “End it quickly, before he hurts anyone.” leads to Find Veit in the woods and end it.

3. Gather herbs for the blessing and bring them to Sister Walburga.

  • Bring 4 Herbs to Sister Walburga: bring 4 × Herb to Sister Walburga.

Hand it in to Hunter Florian. “He woke this morning as himself. I can't thank you enough. I won't forget this.”

Reward: 180 gold, 1200 experience, 3 × Beast Tonic

4. Find Veit in the woods and end it.

  • Find the werewolf that was Veit: defeat 1 × Werewolf.

Hand it in to Hunter Florian. “It's done, then. He would have thanked you. Take this. I can't look at it any more.”

Reward: 320 gold, 1200 experience
