Whispers in the Ruins

Whispers in the Ruins
KindStory
Level10
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartRanger Sylas in Elderglade
RequirementsIll Omens at Millbrook
Hand inRanger Sylas
RewardsHermit: 150 gold, 650 experience, 3 × Blue Potion
Ranger: 150 gold, 650 experience, 1 × Steel Shield

The light in the Greywood comes from the old ruins. Find its source and decide who should keep it.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Ranger Sylas in Elderglade. “Harlan's watchmen saw it too: a light over the Greywood Ruins. The dead don't rest there any more. Will you go?”

1. Find the Greywood Ruins, south-east of Elderglade.

  • Reach the Greywood Ruins: go to Greywood Ruins.

2. The dead walk among the stones. Lay them to rest.

  • Defeat 3 Skeletons: defeat 3 × Skeleton.
  • Defeat the Ghost of the ruins: defeat 1 × Ghost.

3. Under the altar lies a shard of pale stone, warm as a hearth. Hermit Ysolde at Frostpeak Shrine reads the old runes.

  • Show the shard to Hermit Ysolde at Frostpeak Shrine: talk to Hermit Ysolde.

“A shard of the First Star... I have not seen one since I was a girl. It wakes things that should sleep. I could keep it here, under the snow, where nothing lives. Or Sylas could break it on his anvil-stone. It is your find. Choose.”

A choice:

  • “Keep it here, Ysolde. The snow will hold it.” leads to The shard's light drew serpents up the mountain. Drive them off the shrine path.
  • “Sylas should break it. Nothing should keep this.” leads to Dark fairies swarm to the shard's light. Fight your way back to Sylas.

4. The shard's light drew serpents up the mountain. Drive them off the shrine path.

  • Defeat 2 Snow Serpents near the shrine: defeat 2 × Snow Serpent.

Hand it in to Hermit Ysolde. “The shard sleeps. I will watch it, as I have watched everything else.”

Reward: 150 gold, 650 experience, 3 × Blue Potion

5. Dark fairies swarm to the shard's light. Fight your way back to Sylas.

  • Defeat 2 Dark Fairies drawn to the shard: defeat 2 × Dark Fairy.

Hand it in to Ranger Sylas. “There. Ten pieces, and not one of them glowing. Take this; you'll want it.”

Reward: 150 gold, 650 experience, 1 × Steel Shield

Consequences

What you choose here decides which of these you are offered later:
