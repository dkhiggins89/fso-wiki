Daily: Millbrook Watch

Daily: Millbrook Watch
KindDaily (once a day)
Level7
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartReeve Harlan in Millbrook
RequirementsIll Omens at Millbrook
Hand inReeve Harlan
Rewards40 gold

Help the Millbrook watch keep the wolves away from the farms.

A daily quest: it can be done once a day, and comes back the next day (UTC).

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Reeve Harlan in Millbrook. “The watch could use another blade tonight.”
  2. Defeat 5 Wolves: defeat 5 × Wolf.
  3. Return to Reeve Harlan. “Another quiet night, thanks to you.”
