The Lights Below Stonecrest

The Lights Below Stonecrest
KindStory
Level9
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartForeman Durgan in Stonecrest Mine
RequirementsWhispers in the Ruins
Hand inForeman Durgan
RewardsSealed: 160 gold, 850 experience, 4 × Iron Bar
Dug: 300 gold, 850 experience, 3 × Chunk of Silver

The same pale light now shines in the deep gallery of Stonecrest Mine, and the dwarves have gone strange.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Foreman Durgan in Stonecrest Mine. “My lads dug into a seam that glows. Since then the dwarves below won't speak, only swing. Help me.”

1. Go down into the lower gallery of Stonecrest Mine.

  • Enter the Lower Gallery: go to Stonecrest Mine: Lower Gallery.

2. The glowing seam has maddened the dwarves. Subdue them.

  • Subdue 3 maddened Dwarves: defeat 3 × Dwarf.

3. Mine ordinary iron to shore up the gallery while Durgan decides what to do.

  • Mine 5 Chunks of Iron: gather 5 × Chunk of Iron with a skill (what is already in your bag does not count).

4. Tell Durgan what you saw in the glowing seam.

  • Report to Foreman Durgan: talk to Foreman Durgan.

“So it's the same light as the ruins. I could seal the deep gallery and lose the best seam we've ever found. Or we keep digging and I post guards. What would you do?”

A choice:

  • “Seal it. No ore is worth that.” leads to Bring the iron to Garrick the Smith in Millbrook for the seal's bands.
  • “Keep digging, but carefully.” leads to Guards are posted. Clear the ogres that keep raiding the mine road.

5. Bring the iron to Garrick the Smith in Millbrook for the seal's bands.

  • Bring 5 Chunks of Iron to Garrick: bring 5 × Chunk of Iron to Garrick the Smith.

Hand it in to Foreman Durgan. “Sealed tight. The lads grumble, but they sleep.”

Reward: 160 gold, 850 experience, 4 × Iron Bar

6. Guards are posted. Clear the ogres that keep raiding the mine road.

  • Defeat 2 Ogres on the mine road: defeat 2 × Ogre.

Hand it in to Foreman Durgan. “Rich seam, steady guards. Here's your share.”

Reward: 300 gold, 850 experience, 3 × Chunk of Silver
