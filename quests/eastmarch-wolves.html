Wolves at the Hen House

Wolves at the Hen House

Drive off 4 Wolves from the farms of Eastmarch.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Farmer Odo in Millbrook Farms. “You've the look of someone who can swing a sword. The wolves come out of the Greywood every night for my hens. Thin them out for me, would you? Four should teach the rest.”
  2. Defeat Wolves around Eastmarch: defeat 4 × Wolf.
  3. Return to Farmer Odo. “Quiet nights at last! Take a couple of Bess's pies, on me.”
