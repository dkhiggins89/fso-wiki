The Folk of Elderglade

The Folk of Elderglade
KindRegion
Level9
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartRanger Sylas in Elderglade
RequirementsIll Omens at Millbrook
Hand inRanger Sylas
RewardsFight: 130 gold, 3 × Red Potion
Peace: 60 gold, 4 × Monster Treat, 2 × Blue Potion

The elves of Elderglade have turned on travellers. Drive them off, or make peace with an offering.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Ranger Sylas in Elderglade. “The elves were never friendly, but now they shoot at anyone on the path. Go and see why.”

1. Go to Elderglade and see what has stirred the elves.

  • Reach Elderglade: go to Elderglade.

2. The elves' shrine has been defiled. Tell Sylas what you think should be done.

  • Tell Ranger Sylas what you found: talk to Ranger Sylas.

“Defiled? Then they think we did it. We can drive them out, or leave an offering of herbs at the shrine and hope they understand. Your call.”

A choice:

  • “Drive them out. The road must be safe.” leads to Drive the elves and their fairy allies out of Elderglade.
  • “Leave an offering. This was never their fault.” leads to Bring herbs to the shrine in Elderglade as an offering.

3. Drive the elves and their fairy allies out of Elderglade.

  • Defeat 4 Elves: defeat 4 × Elf.
  • Defeat a Dark Fairy: defeat 1 × Dark Fairy.

Hand it in to Ranger Sylas. “Harsh, but the road is ours again.”

Reward: 130 gold, 3 × Red Potion

4. Bring herbs to the shrine in Elderglade as an offering.

  • Gather 4 Herbs: have 4 × Herb with you when you hand it in.
  • Lay them at Elderglade: go to Elderglade.

Hand it in to Ranger Sylas. “They let me pass this morning, and one of them nodded. Thank you.”

Reward: 60 gold, 4 × Monster Treat, 2 × Blue Potion
