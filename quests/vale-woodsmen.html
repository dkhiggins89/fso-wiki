Clear the Logging Road

Clear the Logging Road
KindRegion
Level9
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartWoodcutter Bram in Woodcutters' Camp
RequirementsWelcome to Wrenford
Hand inWoodcutter Bram
Rewards70 gold

Orcs keep raiding the woodcutters' camp, and Bram is short of timber.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Woodcutter Bram in Woodcutters' Camp. “Orcs on the logging road, and my lads won't go out. Cut me some timber and teach those orcs a lesson.”
  2. Cut 8 Logs: gather 8 × Logs with a skill (what is already in your bag does not count).
  3. Defeat 3 Orcs near the camp: defeat 3 × Orc.
  4. Return to Woodcutter Bram. “The road's open and the stacks are full. Good work.”
