Shadows over Alvarena

Drive off 4 Vultures from the golden hills.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Mayor Encarna in Alvarena. “The vultures circle the village day and night now. The goats won't graze and the children won't play. Four of them, please.”
  2. Defeat Vultures in the Golden Hills: defeat 4 × Vulture.
  3. Return to Mayor Encarna. “Blue sky over Alvarena again. Take a cake; Rosa baked it.”
