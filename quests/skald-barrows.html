The Restless Kings

The Restless Kings
StartMother Hild in Vindholm Stave Church
RequirementsOver the Pass
Hand inMother Hild
Rewards180 gold
220 experience
4 × Blue Potion

Lay 5 Draugr of Hauglund to rest.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Mother Hild in Vindholm Stave Church. “The barrow dead have been walking since the frost came early. Five of them, and the moor may settle. Will you go?”
  2. Defeat Draugr on Hauglund Moor: defeat 5 × Draugr.
  3. Return to Mother Hild. “The moor is quieter. Take these, and come back whole next time.”
