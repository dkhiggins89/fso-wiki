Nettle's Remedy

Nettle's Remedy
KindSide quest
Level8
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartMother Nettle in Nettle's Hut
RequirementsThe Drowned Dead
Hand inMother Nettle
RewardsKind: 30 gold, 3 × Monster Treat
Paid: 140 gold

Mother Nettle can cure the fisherman's fever, if you fetch what she needs and see the cure delivered.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Mother Nettle in Nettle's Hut. “Elias has the marsh fever. I can brew the cure: herbs, slime gel, and the heart of a snared loah.”

1. Gather what Mother Nettle needs for the fever cure.

  • Bring 3 Herbs: have 3 × Herb with you when you hand it in.
  • Bring 3 Slime Gel: have 3 × Slime Gel with you when you hand it in.
  • Defeat a Snared Loah: defeat 1 × Snared Loah.

2. Take the cure to Elias the Fisherman.

  • Bring the cure to Elias the Fisherman: talk to Elias the Fisherman.

“Is that... the cure? I can't pay much. A fisherman's purse is light.”

A choice:

  • “Keep your coin. Get well.” leads to Tell Mother Nettle Elias is mending.
  • “Nettle's work isn't free. Pay what you can.” leads to Give Mother Nettle her share of Elias's coin.

3. Tell Mother Nettle Elias is mending.

Hand it in to Mother Nettle. “Kindness is the best medicine I know. Take these treats; the fen beasts like them.”

Reward: 30 gold, 3 × Monster Treat

4. Give Mother Nettle her share of Elias's coin.

Hand it in to Mother Nettle. “Business is business. Here's your cut.”

Reward: 140 gold
