Meat for the Market

Meat for the Market
KindSide quest
Level15
RegionEisenwald
StartButcher Gustav in Hohenburg Market
RequirementsThe Iron Wood
Hand inButcher Gustav
Rewards120 gold

Gustav's stall needs boar and pork before market day.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Butcher Gustav in Hohenburg Market. “Market's tomorrow and my hooks are empty. Boar from the woods, pork from wherever you find it.”
  2. Defeat 5 Wild Boars: defeat 5 × Wild Boar.
  3. Bring 2 Pig: have 2 × Pig with you when you hand it in.
  4. Return to Butcher Gustav. “Fine cuts. Here's your coin.”
