The Ninth Summer

The Ninth Summer
KindSide quest
Level23
RegionSoleira
StartMerchant Baltasar in Corcova
RequirementsInto the Sun
Hand inMerchant Baltasar
Rewards120 gold
140 experience
2 × Olive Oil

Strip 6 Cork from the cork oaks around Corcova.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Merchant Baltasar in Corcova. “This is a ninth summer: the oaks are ready, and my cutters are short-handed. Strip six good sheets of cork for me. An axe and a gentle hand, mind.”
  2. Bring 6 Cork: have 6 × Cork with you when you hand it in.
  3. Return to Merchant Baltasar. “Fine cork, cleanly cut. Take some oil; my cousin presses it.”

Rewards
