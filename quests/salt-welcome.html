Find Captain Morwen on the quay of Port Saltreach.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Harbourmaster Quill in Harbourmaster's House. “You came down the King's Road? Then you've legs. Find Captain Morwen on the quay and ask when the Gull sails.”
  2. Speak with Captain Morwen on the quay: talk to Captain Morwen.
  3. Return to Harbourmaster Quill. “Next tide, then. Good. Here, for your trouble.”
