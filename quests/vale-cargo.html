Lost Cargo

Lost Cargo
KindSide quest
Level13
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartMerchant Ilsa in Saltreach Harbour
RequirementsWord for the Captain
Hand inMerchant Ilsa
Rewards150 gold, 2 × Beast Tonic

Lizard warriors took one of Ilsa's crates. Teli the Watch saw where they went.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Merchant Ilsa in Saltreach Harbour. “A whole crate of my best goods, gone off a cart. Teli on the watch saw who took it.”

1. Ask Teli the Watch what she saw.

  • Ask Teli the Watch: talk to Teli the Watch.

2. Teli saw lizard warriors drag the crate along the shore. Get it back.

  • Defeat 3 Lizard Warriors on the shore: defeat 3 × Lizard Warrior.

Hand it in to Merchant Ilsa. “My crate! Mostly intact. Here.”

Reward: 150 gold, 2 × Beast Tonic
