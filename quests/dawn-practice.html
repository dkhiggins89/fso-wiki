Sword Practice

Sword Practice
StartSergeant Holt in Dawnrest Training Green
RequirementsA New Dawn
Hand inSergeant Holt
Rewards25 gold
30 experience
1 × Short Sword
1 × Small Shield

Defeat 3 Slimes in the practice pen.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Sergeant Holt in Dawnrest Training Green. “So the Elder sent you. The Whisperwood isn't kind to the unprepared. There are three Slimes in my pen; clear them out. Face one and press Space to swing, or just click it.”
  2. Defeat Slimes in the pen: defeat 3 × Slime.
  3. Return to Sergeant Holt. “Not bad at all! Take this sword and shield; they're yours. The gate to the Whisperwood is open now. Follow the path east and you'll reach Wrenford.”

Rewards

  • 25 gold
  • 30 experience
  • 1 × Short Sword
  • 1 × Small Shield
