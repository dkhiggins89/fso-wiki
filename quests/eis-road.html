The Iron Wood

The Iron Wood

Find Burgomaster Friedrich in Hohenburg's town hall.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Border Warden Konrad in Grenzstein. “A traveller from the west! The Burgomaster will want to meet you. Follow the road east to Hohenburg; the town hall is on the market square.”
  2. Speak with Burgomaster Friedrich in Hohenburg: talk to Burgomaster Friedrich.
  3. Return to Border Warden Konrad. “Already met him? Good. Hohenburg needs every pair of hands.”
