Frog Venom

Frog Venom
StartWren the Herbalist in Wren's Cottage
RequirementsSword Practice
Hand inWren the Herbalist
Rewards30 gold
40 experience
2 × Blue Potion

Defeat 2 Poison Frogs at Mossy Pond for Wren's remedies.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Wren the Herbalist in Wren's Cottage. “A traveller! Would you help an old herbalist? The Poison Frogs at Mossy Pond carry the venom I need for my antidotes. The pond is west of here, through the trees.”
  2. Defeat Poison Frogs at Mossy Pond: defeat 2 × Poison Frog.
  3. Return to Wren the Herbalist. “Wonderful! Here, take two of my potions; drink one if a fight goes badly. Wrenford is just over the bridge to the east.”

Rewards

  • 30 gold
  • 40 experience
  • 2 × Blue Potion
