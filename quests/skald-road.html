Find Jarl Sigrun in her hall at Vindholm.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Gatewarden Brynja in Skaldmark Gate. “You came over the pass? Then the Jarl will want to see you. Follow the road north through the Grenwood, then north-east to Vindholm at the head of the fjord.”
  2. Speak with Jarl Sigrun in Vindholm: talk to Jarl Sigrun.
  3. Return to Gatewarden Brynja. “So she has a task for you already. That's the Jarl.”
