The Winged Serpent

The Winged Serpent
KindStory

Hand inDuchess Leonor

Slay the Cuelebre in its hollow in the sierra.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Duchess Leonor in The Alcazaba of Castamar. “The bulls and the vultures were only its shadow. The Cuelebre has taken a village's worth of gold into its cave in the north-west sierra, and it grows bolder every summer. End it.”
  2. Slay the Cuelebre in the Cuelebre's Hollow: defeat 1 × Cuelebre.
  3. Return to Duchess Leonor. “The sierra is free. This crown was my grandmother's; she would want it worn by someone brave.”
