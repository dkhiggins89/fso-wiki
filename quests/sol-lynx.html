Eyes in the Cork Woods

Eyes in the Cork Woods
KindRegion
Level23
RegionSoleira
StartGuard Rodrigo in Corcova
RequirementsInto the Sun
Hand inGuard Rodrigo
Rewards140 gold
170 experience
3 × Monster Treat

Drive off 3 Iberian Lynx from the woods around Corcova.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Guard Rodrigo in Corcova. “The lynx have been taking the cutters' lunches, and lately the cutters' fingers. Three of them, and the woods may calm down. Or tame one, if you have the patience.”
  2. Defeat Iberian Lynx in the Dehesa: defeat 3 × Iberian Lynx.
  3. Return to Guard Rodrigo. “Quiet woods again. Take these treats; you'll want them if you ever tame one.”
