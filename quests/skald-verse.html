The Skald's Lost Verse

The Skald's Lost Verse
KindStory
Level34
RegionSkaldmark
StartSkald Eirik in The Mead House
RequirementsOver the Pass
Hand inSkald Eirik
RewardsTrue: 300 gold, 2100 experience, 1 × Rune Charm
Buried: 600 gold, 2100 experience

Skald Eirik lost the last verse of the Saga of Hauglund. The draugr of the barrows may remember it.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Skald Eirik in The Mead House. “Every skald in Vindholm knows the Saga of Hauglund, but nobody knows the last verse. The draugr in the barrows were there. Make them talk, or make them quiet.”

1. Go to Hauglund Barrows.

  • Reach Hauglund Barrows: go to Hauglund Barrows.

2. The draugr will not speak while their guards stand. Bring them down.

  • Defeat 3 Draugr: defeat 3 × Draugr.

3. The last draugr whispers the verse: it tells of a jarl who betrayed his own hall. Tell Eirik.

  • Tell Skald Eirik the verse: talk to Skald Eirik.

“A betrayal? Jarl Sigrun's line comes from that hall. If I sing this, the hall will be in uproar. Should I sing the saga true, or end it where it always ended?”

A choice:

  • “Sing it true. A saga is no saga with lies in it.” leads to Eirik sings the true verse in the Mead House.
  • “Let the old ending stand. Some things are better buried.” leads to The Jarl hears you kept the secret and sends her thanks.

4. Eirik sings the true verse in the Mead House.

Hand it in to Skald Eirik. “The hall is shouting, but the saga is whole. That's what skalds are for.”

Reward: 300 gold, 2100 experience, 1 × Rune Charm

5. The Jarl hears you kept the secret and sends her thanks.

Hand it in to Jarl Sigrun. “Eirik told me what you chose. My family owes you. Take this, and say nothing.”

Reward: 600 gold, 2100 experience
