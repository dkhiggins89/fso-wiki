The Hermit's Vigil

The Hermit's Vigil
KindStory
Level26
RegionSoleira
StartHermit Ramon in Hermitage of San Ramon
RequirementsInto the Sun
Hand inHermit Ramon
RewardsTruth: 220 gold, 1600 experience, 4 × Blue Potion
Lie: 450 gold, 1600 experience

Hermit Ramon keeps a vigil for a lost pilgrim. Find her, and decide what to tell him.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Hermit Ramon in Hermitage of San Ramon. “A pilgrim girl, Alba, left for the sierra a week ago and has not come back. I pray, but prayer has no legs. Will you look?”

1. Follow the pilgrim's track into the sierra. The lynxes there are bold.

  • Defeat 3 Iberian Lynxes on the track: defeat 3 × Iberian Lynx.

2. Vultures circle a ravine. Drive them off and see what they guard.

  • Defeat 3 Vultures: defeat 3 × Vulture.

3. You found Alba, alive, living with goatherds in the hills. She does not want to go back.

  • Return to Hermit Ramon: talk to Hermit Ramon.

“You found her? Tell me. Is she well? Is she coming home?”

A choice:

  • “She's safe and happy, but she isn't coming back.” leads to Ramon has a gift for you.
  • “She's on her way. Give her time.” leads to Ramon presses a heavy purse into your hand.

4. Ramon has a gift for you.

Hand it in to Hermit Ramon. “Safe and happy. That is more than I prayed for. Thank you for the truth.”

Reward: 220 gold, 1600 experience, 4 × Blue Potion

5. Ramon presses a heavy purse into your hand.

Hand it in to Hermit Ramon. “Then I will keep the candle lit until she comes. Take this, for her journey.”

Reward: 450 gold, 1600 experience
