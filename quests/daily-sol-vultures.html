Daily: Vulture Watch

Daily: Vulture Watch
KindDaily (once a day)
Level26
RegionSoleira
StartGuard Diego in Castamar South Gate
RequirementsInto the Sun

Rewards130 gold

Vultures harry the caravans on the southern road.

A daily quest: it can be done once a day, and comes back the next day (UTC).

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Guard Diego in Castamar South Gate. “The vultures follow the caravans waiting for something to die. Make it them.”
  2. Defeat 6 Vultures: defeat 6 × Vulture.
  3. Return to Guard Diego. “Good hunting. Tomorrow?”
