Daily: Slime Patrol

Daily: Slime Patrol
KindDaily (once a day)
Level3
RegionWrenford Vale
StartTeral the Guard in Wrenford Gate
RequirementsSword Practice
Hand inTeral the Guard
Rewards20 gold

Slimes keep creeping onto the roads. Thin them out, every day.

A daily quest: it can be done once a day, and comes back the next day (UTC).

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Teral the Guard in Wrenford Gate. “Slimes on the road again. Same as yesterday. Same as tomorrow.”
  2. Defeat 6 Slimes: defeat 6 × Slime.
  3. Return to Teral the Guard. “Roads are clear. Back tomorrow?”

Rewards

  • 20 gold
