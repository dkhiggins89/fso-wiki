Trouble in the Hollow

Trouble in the Hollow
StartRanger Sylas in Elderglade
RequirementsWolves at the Hen House
Hand inRanger Sylas
Rewards150 gold
180 experience
2 × Beast Tonic

Defeat 5 Orcs in the Greywood, and the Orc Lord of Orc Hollow.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Ranger Sylas in Elderglade. “The orcs have dug in at the Hollow, north-east of here, and their lord sends raiders down to the King's Road. I can't hold them alone. Will you help?”
  2. Defeat Orcs in the Greywood: defeat 5 × Orc.
  3. Defeat the Orc Lord in Orc Hollow: defeat 1 × Orc Lord.
  4. Return to Ranger Sylas. “The road's safer already. You've earned this, and my thanks.”

Rewards

  • 150 gold
  • 180 experience
  • 2 × Beast Tonic
