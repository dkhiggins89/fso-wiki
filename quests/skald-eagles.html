Thieves on the Wind

Drive off 4 Sea Eagles from Isvik.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Old Halvard in Isvik. “Every fish I pull through the ice, an eagle has it before it's stopped flapping. Four of the thieving birds, and I'll eat again.”
  2. Defeat Sea Eagles in the north: defeat 4 × Sea Eagle.
  3. Return to Old Halvard. “Ha! Char for supper. Have some yourself.”
