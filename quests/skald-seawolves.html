The Sea Wolves

The Sea Wolves
StartHarbourmaster Bjarne in Ravnsund
RequirementsOver the Pass
Hand inHarbourmaster Bjarne
Rewards400 gold
600 experience
1 × Huscarl Shield

Break the Sea Wolves at Ulfsnes: defeat 4 raiders and their chieftain.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Harbourmaster Bjarne in Ravnsund. “See the smoke across the water? Sea Wolves. They took two of our boats this month and they'll be back for the toll house. Break them, and the Jarl's shieldsmith will see you right.”
  2. Defeat Sea Wolf Reavers: defeat 4 × Sea Wolf Reaver.
  3. Defeat the Sea Wolf Chieftain at Ulfsnes: defeat 1 × Sea Wolf Chieftain.
  4. Return to Harbourmaster Bjarne. “No smoke at Ulfsnes this morning. Take this shield; it was made for a huscarl, and you've earned it.”
