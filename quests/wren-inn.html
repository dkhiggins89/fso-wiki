Supper at the Brook & Barrel

Supper at the Brook & Barrel
KindSide quest
Level3
RegionWrenford Vale
StartRosa the Innkeeper in The Brook & Barrel
RequirementsSword Practice
Hand inRosa the Innkeeper
Rewards20 gold
2 × Pie

Rosa needs apples and milk for tonight's supper.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Rosa the Innkeeper in The Brook & Barrel. “Apple pie and a jug of milk, that's what the regulars want. Help a girl out?”
  2. Bring 2 Apples: have 2 × Apple with you when you hand it in.
  3. Bring 1 Milk: have 1 × Milk with you when you hand it in.
  4. Return to Rosa the Innkeeper. “Supper is saved! Sit down, have a slice.”

Rewards

  • 20 gold
