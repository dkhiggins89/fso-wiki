Silver for the Countess

Silver for the Countess
StartAssayer Klara in Kesselbach Smelter
RequirementsTrouble Below
Hand inAssayer Klara
Rewards260 gold
240 experience
2 × Silver Bar

Bring Assayer Klara 5 Chunks of Silver from the Kesselbach veins.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Assayer Klara in Kesselbach Smelter. “The Countess's tithe is due and the mine has been shut for a month. Five chunks of good silver from the veins around town, please.”
  2. Bring 5 Chunks of Silver
  3. Return to Assayer Klara. “Bright and clean. The tithe is paid, and here's your share.”

Rewards

  • 260 gold
  • 240 experience
  • 2 × Silver Bar
