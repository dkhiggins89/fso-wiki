The Big One

The Big One
KindSide quest
Level26
RegionSoleira
StartFisher Lalo in Puerto Dorado Beach
RequirementsInto the Sun
Hand inFisher Lalo
Rewards150 gold
1 × Fishing Net

Fisher Lalo wants proof the sea still gives.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Fisher Lalo in Puerto Dorado Beach. “Forty years on this beach and the fish are getting smaller. Prove me wrong.”
  2. Catch 8 Raw Fish: gather 8 × Raw Fish with a skill (what is already in your bag does not count).
  3. Return to Fisher Lalo. “Ha! The sea's still got it. Here.”

Rewards

  • 150 gold
