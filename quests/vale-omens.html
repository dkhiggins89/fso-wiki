Ill Omens at Millbrook

Ill Omens at Millbrook
KindStory
Level6
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartReeve Harlan in Millbrook
RequirementsWelcome to Wrenford
Hand inReeve Harlan
RewardsTold: 80 gold, 300 experience, 2 × Red Potion
Quiet: 40 gold, 300 experience, 1 × Rune Charm

Something is killing Millbrook's cattle and the shepherds speak of lights at the Ninestones.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Reeve Harlan in Millbrook. “Three cows dead in a week, and not a wolf track that makes sense. Odo found them. Will you look into it?”

1. Farmer Odo found the dead cattle. Hear what he saw.

  • Ask Farmer Odo about the cattle: talk to Farmer Odo.

2. Odo blames wolves, though the wounds were strange. Thin out the wolves around Millbrook.

  • Defeat 4 Wolves around Millbrook: defeat 4 × Wolf.

3. The wolves were only scavenging. Shepherd Aldric at the Ninestones may know more.

  • Speak with Shepherd Aldric at the Ninestones: talk to Shepherd Aldric.

“I've seen it three nights running: a pale light rising from the Greywood, east, like a star that fell and never went out. The herds bolt from it. Will you tell the Reeve? Folk will panic.”

A choice:

  • “Harlan should know. I'll tell him everything.” leads to Tell Reeve Harlan about the light in the Greywood.
  • “Then we keep it between us until we know more.” leads to Return to Shepherd Aldric; he has something for keeping his secret.

4. Tell Reeve Harlan about the light in the Greywood.

Hand it in to Reeve Harlan. “A falling light? I'll post a watch on the east road. Ranger Sylas should hear of this too.”

Reward: 80 gold, 300 experience, 2 × Red Potion

5. Return to Shepherd Aldric; he has something for keeping his secret.

Hand it in to Shepherd Aldric. “My grandmother carved this. Keep it close, out there in the dark.”

Reward: 40 gold, 300 experience, 1 × Rune Charm
