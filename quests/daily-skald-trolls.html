Daily: Troll Watch

Daily: Troll Watch
KindDaily (once a day)
Level34
RegionSkaldmark
StartGuard Rolf in Vindholm
RequirementsOver the Pass
Hand inGuard Rolf
Rewards200 gold

Frost trolls come down from Trollheim every night.

A daily quest: it can be done once a day, and comes back the next day (UTC).

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Guard Rolf in Vindholm. “Trolls at the walls again. Lend us your arm?”
  2. Defeat 3 Frost Trolls: defeat 3 × Frost Troll.
  3. Return to Guard Rolf. “Good work. They'll be back tomorrow.”
