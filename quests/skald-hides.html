Furs for Winter

Furs for Winter
KindSide quest
Level32
RegionSkaldmark
StartHuntress Sigrid in Vindholm Lanes
RequirementsOver the Pass
Hand inHuntress Sigrid
Rewards220 gold

Sigrid is stocking furs before the long dark.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Huntress Sigrid in Vindholm Lanes. “Winter comes early here. I need elk and reindeer hides for the town's cloaks.”
  2. Defeat 3 Elk: defeat 3 × Elk.
  3. Defeat 3 Reindeer: defeat 3 × Reindeer.
  4. Bring 5 Hides: have 5 × Hide with you when you hand it in.
  5. Return to Huntress Sigrid. “Warm cloaks for everyone. Takk.”
