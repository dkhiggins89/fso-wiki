Daily: Fresh Catch

Daily: Fresh Catch
KindDaily (once a day)
Level7
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartFisher Lena in Mirrowford
RequirementsWelcome to Wrenford
Hand inFisher Lena
Rewards40 gold

Lena sells fresh fish at Mirrowford every morning and always needs more.

A daily quest: it can be done once a day, and comes back the next day (UTC).

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Fisher Lena in Mirrowford. “Mirrowford folk want fish every day. Bring me some?”
  2. Catch 5 Raw Fish: gather 5 × Raw Fish with a skill (what is already in your bag does not count).
  3. Return to Fisher Lena. “Lovely and fresh. Same again tomorrow?”
