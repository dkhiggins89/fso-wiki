Ratmas's Lesson

Ratmas's Lesson
KindSide quest
Level3
RegionWrenford Vale
StartRatmas the Fisher in Riverside Cottages
RequirementsSword Practice
Hand inRatmas the Fisher
Rewards15 gold

Ratmas will teach you to fish, if you bring back a catch.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Ratmas the Fisher in Riverside Cottages. “Never fished? Take a pole to the water and bring me three fish. That's the lesson.”
  2. Catch 3 Raw Fish: gather 3 × Raw Fish with a skill (what is already in your bag does not count).
  3. Return to Ratmas the Fisher. “Not bad for a beginner!”
