Daily: Shore Patrol

Daily: Shore Patrol
KindDaily (once a day)
Level12
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartTeli the Watch in Saltreach Harbour
RequirementsWord for the Captain
Hand inTeli the Watch
Rewards70 gold

Keep the lizard warriors off Saltreach's beaches.

A daily quest: it can be done once a day, and comes back the next day (UTC).

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Teli the Watch in Saltreach Harbour. “They come back every tide. Take a turn on the patrol?”
  2. Defeat 4 Lizard Warriors: defeat 4 × Lizard Warrior.
  3. Return to Teli the Watch. “Beaches clear. Same time tomorrow.”
