The Smith's Errand

The Smith's Errand
KindSide quest
Level3
RegionWrenford Vale
StartBrannoc the Smith in Brannoc's Forge
RequirementsSword Practice
Hand inBrannoc the Smith
Rewards25 gold, 1 × Short Sword

Brannoc needs iron ore and a delivery to the guard.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

Talk to Brannoc the Smith in Brannoc's Forge. “Mine me some iron and I'll forge Teral a new blade. You can take it to him.”

1. Mine iron for Brannoc.

  • Mine 3 Chunks of Iron: gather 3 × Chunk of Iron with a skill (what is already in your bag does not count).

2. Bring the iron to Brannoc so he can forge the blade.

  • Give Brannoc the iron: bring 3 × Chunk of Iron to Brannoc the Smith.

3. Tell Teral the Guard his new blade is ready.

  • Tell Teral the Guard: talk to Teral the Guard.

Hand it in to Brannoc the Smith. “Teral will be pleased.”

Reward: 25 gold, 1 × Short Sword
