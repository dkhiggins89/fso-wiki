A Place of Your Own

A Place of Your Own
KindSide quest
Level32
RegionSkaldmark
StartLand Agent Hjalmar in Vindholm Steading: Ravnsgata
RequirementsNone
Hand inLand Agent Hjalmar
Rewards3200 experience
1 × Large House Deed

Land Agent Hjalmar sells plots in Vindholm Steading. First you need a house deed.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Land Agent Hjalmar in Vindholm Steading: Ravnsgata. “Six steads inside the palisade. The jarl grants land only to those who have proved themselves. You'll need a deed first. I can arrange one, if you show me you're serious.”
  2. Reach character level 32.
  3. Bring 30 Logs to Land Agent Hjalmar: bring 30 × Logs to Land Agent Hjalmar.
  4. Return to Land Agent Hjalmar. “Here's your deed. Use it on any free plot in Vindholm Steading and pick the house you want. The plot costs 60,000 gold, and the tax is due every week from your bank.”

Rewards

  • 3200 experience
  • 1 × Large House Deed
