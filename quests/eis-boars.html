Tusks in the Turnips

Tusks in the Turnips
StartFarmer Gertrud in Hohenburg Gardens
RequirementsThe Iron Wood
Hand inFarmer Gertrud
Rewards150 gold
190 experience
3 × Sausages

Drive off 5 Wild Boar from the Wolfbach Valley.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Farmer Gertrud in Hohenburg Gardens. “The boars come out of the beechwood every night and dig up everything I plant. Five of them would teach the rest a lesson.”
  2. Defeat Wild Boar in the valley: defeat 5 × Wild Boar.
  3. Return to Farmer Gertrud. “Quiet fields at last. Gustav will make sausages of the rest.”
