The Carver's Commission

The Carver's Commission
StartWoodcarver Lukas in Tannheim
RequirementsThe Iron Wood
Hand inWoodcarver Lukas
Rewards140 gold
160 experience
1 × Lyre

Bring Woodcarver Lukas 6 Oak Logs.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Woodcarver Lukas in Tannheim. “The Countess wants a carved screen for the great hall, and I'm out of good oak. Six logs, from the oaks of the Beechwood.”
  2. Bring 6 Oak Logs
  3. Return to Woodcarver Lukas. “Fine straight grain. Take this lyre; I carved it last winter.”
