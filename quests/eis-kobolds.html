Trouble Below

Trouble Below
StartMine Foreman Anton in Kesselbach
RequirementsThe Iron Wood
Hand inMine Foreman Anton
Rewards220 gold
260 experience
1 × Steel Pickaxe

Clear 6 Forest Kobolds out of the Kesselbach mine.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Mine Foreman Anton in Kesselbach. “The Kobolds have taken the lower galleries again. They steal the silver, hide the tools and laugh at us in the dark. Clear them out.”
  2. Defeat Forest Kobolds: defeat 6 × Forest Kobold.
  3. Return to Mine Foreman Anton. “Quiet galleries. My miners will go back down tomorrow. Take a good pick.”
