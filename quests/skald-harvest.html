Bread for the Hall

Bread for the Hall
KindSide quest
Level36
RegionSkaldmark
StartFarmer Gudrun in Fjellby
RequirementsOver the Pass
Hand inFarmer Gudrun
Rewards80 gold
90 experience
5 × Rye Seeds
3 × Cloudberry Seeds

Bring Farmer Gudrun 6 Barley.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Farmer Gudrun in Fjellby. “The Jarl's hall eats more bread than the whole valley. My barley's gone short. Grow some for me, or buy some, I don't mind which: six sheaves.”
  2. Bring 6 Barley: have 6 × Barley with you when you hand it in.
  3. Return to Farmer Gudrun. “That'll keep the ovens going. Here: rye and cloudberry seeds, for your own fields.”
