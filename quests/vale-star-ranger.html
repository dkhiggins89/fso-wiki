Heart of the Fallen Star

Heart of the Fallen Star
KindStory
Level13
RegionThe Vale Lands
StartRanger Sylas in Elderglade
RequirementsThe Lights Below Stonecrest
Only if you chose “Sylas should break it. Nothing should keep this.” in Whispers in the Ruins
Hand inRanger Sylas
Rewards400 gold
1500 experience
1 × Dark Shield

Breaking the shard showed Sylas where the rest of the fallen star lies. End it.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Ranger Sylas in Elderglade. “When the shard broke, every piece pointed east. The heart of the fallen star is out there, and it's feeding the worms. Finish it.”
  2. Defeat the Orc Lord who guards the eastern trail: defeat 1 × Orc Lord.
  3. Defeat 2 Mutant Worms feeding on the star's heart: defeat 2 × Mutant Worm.
  4. Reach character level 12.
  5. Return to Ranger Sylas. “The pieces have gone dark. It's over. Take this, with my thanks.”

Rewards

  • 400 gold
  • 1500 experience
  • 1 × Dark Shield
