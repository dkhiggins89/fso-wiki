The Wolf of Tannheim

The Wolf of Tannheim
KindRegion

RegionEisenwald

RequirementsThe Iron Wood
Hand inForester Agnes
Rewards320 gold

Hunt down 2 Werewolves in the deep forest.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Forester Agnes in Tannheim Carvers' Lane. “Something takes the deer and leaves tracks that start as a wolf's and end as a man's. There are two of them, I think. Near the old Wolf Tower.”
  2. Defeat Werewolves in the Eisenwald: defeat 2 × Werewolf.
  3. Return to Forester Agnes. “Tannheim will sleep tonight. Thank you.”

Rewards

  • 320 gold
  • 420 experience
