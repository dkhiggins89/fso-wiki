Bulls on the Road

Bulls on the Road
StartDuchess Leonor in The Alcazaba of Castamar
RequirementsInto the Sun
Hand inDuchess Leonor
Rewards160 gold
200 experience
2 × Paella

Drive off 4 Wild Bulls from the dehesa road.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Duchess Leonor in The Alcazaba of Castamar. “The herds of the dehesa have gone wild since the Cuelebre came down from the sierra. Four carts overturned this month. Drive them off the Corcova road.”
  2. Defeat Wild Bulls in the Dehesa: defeat 4 × Wild Bull.
  3. Return to Duchess Leonor. “The carters are singing your name in the market. Take this, from my own kitchen.”

Rewards

  • 160 gold
  • 200 experience
