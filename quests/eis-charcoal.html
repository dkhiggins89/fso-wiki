Wood for the Kilns

Wood for the Kilns
KindSide quest
Level19
RegionEisenwald
StartCharcoal Burner Ruprecht in The Charcoal Burners' Camp
RequirementsThe Iron Wood
Hand inCharcoal Burner Ruprecht
Rewards140 gold
3 × Iron Bar

The charcoal kilns need oak, and the smiths need charcoal.

Rewards are raised to suit the level of the place the quest is given, so they may be larger than listed.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Charcoal Burner Ruprecht in The Charcoal Burners' Camp. “The smiths want charcoal by the cartload. I need oak, lots of it.”
  2. Cut 6 Oak Logs: gather 6 × Oak Logs with a skill (what is already in your bag does not count).
  3. Return to Charcoal Burner Ruprecht. “That'll keep the kilns going a week.”

Rewards

  • 140 gold
  • 3 × Iron Bar
