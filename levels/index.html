Levels

Defeating monsters and finishing quests gives experience. Each level gives you more health and makes your blows hit harder; a level up also heals you. Your combat skills grow by fighting monsters that match them and unlock better weapons and armour. Monsters have levels too, shown when you point at them, and every region has a band of levels, shown when you enter it. A monster well below your level gives little experience and trains nothing; one above you gives more.
