Beasts

Tame monsters with Taming, keep them at the ranch with Ranching, and fight beast battles. See the guide Beasts and taming.

Elements

  • Fire beats Wind
  • Wind beats Earth
  • Earth beats Water
  • Water beats Fire
  • Light beats Shadow
  • Shadow beats Light

A move of the beast's own element does 25% more.
