The Hearth at Vindholm

The first boats came to Vindholm in a winter so hard that the oars struck ice before they struck water. A raven flew ahead of them and settled on the bare headland. There the rowers raised a turf hall, and there they kept the first hearth alight.

When the starstone fell beyond the fells, the night shone green over the snow. Some called it a warning; others called it a road laid across the sky. The skalds kept both verses. A song that remembers only the brave is a poor account of a winter.

Ravnsund swore to keep the sea road, Fjellby the high paths, and Isvik the fishing lights. Their flags are different, but a traveller may still ask for a place beside any hearth. So the saga says: a realm is held together by the doors it opens.
