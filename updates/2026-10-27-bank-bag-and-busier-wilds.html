Bankers, a bigger bag and busier wilds

2026-10-27

More changes from playing a new character from the start.

  • Bankers in twenty towns. Right click a town's merchant, innkeeper or harbourmaster and choose Bank to open

your Vault and bank your gold. Lir the Merchant does it in Wrenford Market.

  • A bigger bag: 28 different things instead of 15. Older worlds with the original rules keep 15.
  • Quest rewards suit the place. A quest given in a level 30 region now pays like one: at least a quarter of a

level's experience, more for the big ones, and gold to match. Rewards that were already larger stay as they were.

  • More monsters around Wrenford. Fifteen more slimes, wolves, frogs and a pig in the woods, the glade and on the

roads east, so the first levels are not spent looking for something to fight.

  • Masterwork recipes: every top weapon, shield and armour that could not be found anywhere can now be made at an

Anvil with high Blacksmithing and good materials.

  • Sell all sells every one of an item the shop buys, with one click.
  • Monsters come back after 25 seconds instead of 8, so a busy spot is not a queue.
  • Level-up pop-ups only appear when a level opens something new; the experience line still tells you every level.
