Skills overhaul, taming, ranching and beast battles

2026-10-18

Skills

Skills that always trained together are now one skill: Swordsmanship covers the old Slash, Thrust, Fencing, Heavy Arms and Unholy Combat; Unarmed, Defence and Arcane Magic likewise. Your best level in each group carries over. A kill now trains the skill for the weapon you hold. New skills: Tailoring, Fletching, Taming and Ranching.

Crafting chains

Ore becomes bars, bars become weapons, tools and armour; hides become leather, wool becomes cloth; logs and feathers become arrows. See Crafting chains. Bows now need arrows in your off hand.

Taming, ranching and battles

Tame 35 kinds of monster, take a companion with you, keep beasts at the new Wrenford Ranch, breed them, keep pets at home, and battle trainers, the Castle Tournament and other players. See Beasts and taming.
