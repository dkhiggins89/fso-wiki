Eisenwald woodland creatures and the Wild Hunt

2026-10-25

Live since 26 October 2026. This first preview was not approved. Revised designs await review; no new creature has been added to the game.

Eisenwald's forest wildlife is taking shape: a brown bristled Wild Boar and a grey striped Wildcat with a thick ringed tail. Both are intended to become tameable after approval.

The Forest Kobold wears a leather miner's hood and carries a pickaxe and an ore pouch. The Werewolf has a long muzzle, a heavy ruff and large clawed hands.

The Wild Huntsman rides a pale spectral horse, carrying a spear and a hunting horn beneath his flowing cloak. A separate spectral Hunt Hound is ready to accompany him. His night-time encounters and quest will be built with the realm's maps later.

Each design has four standing views and authored idle, walk, attack, hurt and KO movements. These are review candidates only: no new monster has been registered, no combat or taming rules have changed, and existing creature artwork is unchanged. The approved building kit is already in source; Eisenwald's playable maps are still to come. Nothing has been installed or published.

The revised set is described in Eisenwald creatures revised for the forest. It addresses the ears, game-size readability, feline shape, shaggy wolf outline, exposed joints and the Huntsman disappearance.
