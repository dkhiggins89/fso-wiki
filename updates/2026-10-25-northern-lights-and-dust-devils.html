Northern lights and dust devils

2026-10-25

Snowy Skaldmark nights now have soft green and teal curtains across the upper view. The lights sit below the character panel and leave the town lamps readable. La Mancha and the Golden Hills have occasional small tan dust devils by day: sand rises, turns, skims across the ground and fades away.

The effects stay outside interiors and caves. Reduce Motion turns off dust devils and keeps the aurora static and softer. The regional visuals follow HD graphics and lighting settings.

Original synthesized ambience adds gentle surf and occasional gull calls near the harbours of Port Saltreach, Ravnsund, Isvik and Puerto Dorado, plus wind on the Troll Fells and Sierra Parda. All use the existing Ambience volume and mute controls. No downloaded or recorded samples were used.

The approved aurora and unchanged sound samples accompany the new dust-devil capture. Source checks, both regional capture suites and a fresh copied regression are complete. The known older regression exceptions are recorded in the development checkpoint. Nothing has been installed or published.

See Regional atmosphere for controls, and Songs, sagas and Soleira wine and Soleira townsfolk complete for the other regional additions. Release building now returns to Claude.
