Levels that matter

2026-10-26

Until now a level gave one point of health and nothing else, almost every kill gave the same experience, and a Slime trained your sword as well as a dragon. That changes.

You

  • Each level makes you stronger. About 7% more health and 3.5% more damage a level, and a level up heals you.

Characters you already have are brought up to the health of their level when they next log in; nobody loses anything.

  • A new experience table. Early levels come quickly, later ones take longer. Experience carries over, so a big

kill can give more than one level. The full table is on the Levels page.

  • Food and healing spells keep up. They heal the same share of your health as they did at level 1, so a meal

still matters at level 40.

  • No more losing health for dying. The old rule that a death could cost a point of maximum health is gone.

Monsters

  • Monsters have levels. Point at one or right click it to see its level beside its rating. A Wolf at the edge of

Wrenford is a very different beast from a Wolf deep in Skaldmark.

  • Every region has a band, shown when you walk in: Wrenford Vale 1-5, the Vale Lands 5-14, Eisenwald 12-24,

Soleira 20-32 and Skaldmark 28-42. Monsters are at the low end where you come in and grow stronger the further you go.

  • Fighting your match pays. A monster well below you gives little experience (a tenth once it is eight levels

down); one above you gives more. Bosses give eight times as much.

  • Skills grow against a match too. Your weapon skill and Block now grow from fights with monsters strong enough

to teach you something, so to reach the best weapons and armour you have to go where the danger is. Block also grows from blows you take while wearing armour.

  • Bosses, rare monsters, invaders and the Sunken Crypt are levelled to match: bosses a little above their

region's band, rares and invaders a little above where they appear, and the Crypt to the strongest member of your party.
