Soleira - the realm of the sun

2026-10-23

South of the Fenmire's reeds lies Soleira, a sun-baked realm of white villages, cork oak pasture, olive and orange groves and dry golden plains. It is 240 maps, the size of Skaldmark.

Getting there

  • On foot: walk south from the Drowned Chapel in the Fenmire, through the gap in its southern reeds. You arrive at

Fenmire Crossing, where Border Warden Ines keeps watch.

  • By sea: Captain Morwen on the Port Saltreach quay sails to Puerto Dorado, and Captain Soledad sails back.

Coaches link Castamar, Corcova, Alvarena and Puerto Dorado, and each of those towns has a starstone.

The land is split into the Sierra Parda (pine and rock), the Dehesa (cork oak pasture), Olivar, La Mancha, the Golden Hills, the Orange Coast and the Atlantic Shore. The Rio Dorado runs from the north-east to the sea west of Puerto Dorado, with stone bridges where the roads cross it. Soleira has its own music, a Spanish guitar-like tune.

Creatures

  • Wild Bull: charges across the dehesa and the plain.
  • Iberian Lynx: hunts among the cork oaks and in the hills.
  • Vulture: circles over the hills and the plain.
  • Cuelebre: a winged serpent. It is a boss in its hollow: it strikes a ring of ground around itself (watch for

the red warning) and calls Vultures to help when it is hurt.

You can tame the Wild Bull, the Lynx and the Vulture with a Monster Treat.

Farming and gathering

  • New crops: Saffron (Farming 40), Olives (Farming 25) and Oranges (Farming 18). Their seeds are sold by Fruit

Seller Carmen and Gardener Rosario in Castamar and by Farmer Dolores in the saffron fields.

  • Cork oaks (Lumberjacking 20): strip them with an axe for Cork. The bark grows back in time.
  • New cooking: Olive Oil (Cooking 25), Orange Cake (Cooking 30) and Paella (Cooking 45).

Deeds, feats and a festival

  • Deeds of Soleira: Novice, Adept and Master. The Master deeds give the title "of the Sun".
  • Feats: Serpentslayer (slay the Cuelebre 3 times) and Bull Runner (defeat 50 Wild Bulls).
