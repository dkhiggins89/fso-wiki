Faster live map editing and smoother reconnects

2026-10-18

For world builders

Editing the live world with the map editor (F10) is much faster:

  • The server used to freeze for several seconds on every save, long enough to drop your connection. A save now takes

well under a second.

  • Your strokes are collected and saved together a moment after you stop painting, instead of one save per stroke.
  • You can keep painting while a save is on its way; those strokes go in the next save.
  • Undoing a stroke that has not been saved yet happens at once. To undo an already-saved change, any unsaved strokes

are sent first, then press Ctrl+Z again.

For everyone

If your connection drops, the game reconnects you straight away. Before, the server could still think you were logged in and refuse with "already logged in" until it noticed the old connection was gone.
