Eating when full, and rarer potions

2026-10-28

  • Food when you are full. It used to refuse with "too full". Now it still restores half of its usual health and

energy (it adds no hunger) and is used up.

  • Potions always work in full. Whatever your hunger, a potion restores everything it should.
  • Potions are rare and expensive. Shops now charge from 150 gold (Blue) up to 1000 (White) and keep only three in

stock, so keep them for the moments that matter. The item tooltips say how they behave when you are full.
