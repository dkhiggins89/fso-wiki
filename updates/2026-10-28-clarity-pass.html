A clarity pass on the interface

2026-10-28

A walk through every window turned up places where text was hard to see or in the way:

  • Quest log. Story, region, side and daily quests used dark inks meant for the old parchment window, which vanished

on the dark glass. They are now light colours, and the small detail lines are larger.

  • Deeds and guild. "Reward: ..." lines in the Deeds window and the guild message of the day are bright gold instead

of dark brown.

  • Skills window. It opens clear of the status panel, so its title is no longer covered.
  • Help (F1). Each key now sits level with the first line of its description.
  • Star Chart, headings, exits, lamps. See the other updates today for the Star Chart text, the heading font, exits

hidden by the action bar and the larger lamps and fountain.
