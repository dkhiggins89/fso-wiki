Trees, ore and fishing spots

2026-10-22

Gathering has grown up. Every tree, rock and fishing spot now has a kind of its own, with a level to work it and something to show for it.

Fishing spots

Open water still gives Raw Fish, Trout and Catfish to a Fishing Pole. You can now also fish from a bank or shore, not only from open water. Look out for fishing spots, each needing its own tool:

  • River ripples (Fly Rod, a Feather for each catch): Trout, Salmon.
  • Lake shoals on Mirrowmere and the ponds (Fishing Net): Raw Fish, Perch, Pike.
  • Fen pools in the Fenmire (Fishing Pole): Eel, Catfish.
  • Sea shoals off the Saltreach Coast (Harpoon): Mackerel, Tuna, Swordfish, Squid, and at night the rare Starfin.
  • Ice holes on Frostpeak's snowfields (Fishing Pole): Arctic Char.

A fished-out spot goes calm for a while, then comes back.

Better tools

Each tool needs a level to gather with. Better tools work faster:

  • Axes: Axe, Steel Axe (20), Gold Axe (35), Titanium Axe (55), Star Axe (75).
  • Picks: Mining Hammer, Steel Pickaxe (20), Gold Pickaxe (35), Titanium Pickaxe (55), Star Pickaxe (75).
  • Fishing: Fishing Pole, Fishing Net (5), Fly Rod (20), Harpoon (40), and the Star Rod (75), which works at every spot.

Right-click a tree, rock or spot and you'll use the best fitting tool in your bag. Smiths, the woodcutter, the mine foreman and the fishmongers sell the first tiers. They also buy logs, ore, bars and fish.

Smelting and smithing

Smelting moves to the new Furnace, which stands beside every anvil. It makes Iron, Silver, Steel, Gold, Titanium and Star Bars. At the anvil you can forge:

  • every new axe and pick, and the Harpoon;
  • the Gold, Ice and Fire weapons and armour;
  • Titanium Armor, from Titanium Bars;
  • rings, which now use Silver and Gold Bars.

Elsewhere, Fletching makes the Fly Rod and Star Rod, Tailoring makes the Fishing Net, and the new catches cook into eight new dishes, up to the Starfin Platter.
