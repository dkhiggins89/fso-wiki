Sit and rest, punchier hits and handier hover hints

2026-10-27

  • Sit and rest. Type /sit, press H, or right click yourself and choose Sit down. Sitting starts your

recovery two seconds after a fight instead of six, and heals twice as fast. Taking a step or a blow stands you up; you cannot sit down in the middle of a fight. Other players see you sitting.

  • Hits feel heavier. A struck monster flinches for a moment and is pushed back a little (a critical hit pushes

further). Turn on Reduce motion in Settings to keep monsters still.

  • Your companion's blows show their damage in teal, so you can tell them from your own.
  • Gathering hints. Hover a tree, rock or fishing spot to see the skill level it needs beside yours: green when you

can work it, red when you cannot yet.

  • Compact status panel. Settings > Interface > Compact status panel shrinks the panel at the top left to your

name and level, one line of numbers, the bars and your gold.

  • H can be changed in Settings > Controls like any other key.
