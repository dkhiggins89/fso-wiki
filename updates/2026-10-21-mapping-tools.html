Map templates, region names, big things in one click and houses you can walk behind

2026-10-21

Walking behind buildings

The roofs of the Thatched cottage, Tavern, Blacksmith's forge, Village church and Castle are now drawn over characters, like tree crowns. You can walk along the back of a building and see yourself through the roof, instead of being stopped by an invisible wall. The front walls still block, and doors still lead inside. Buildings already standing in the world have been changed the same way, and so have the layouts in the map editor.

For mappers

When you make a new area, or a new section to the north, east, south or west, you can start from a ready-made layout instead of plain grass:

Pick one under New sections start as in the Map tab, or as the style of a new area. Each one is a little different every time, the shores and path edges are fitted automatically, and you can always walk from one side of the map to the other (the beach and lake from side to side).

Region names

Not every map needs its own name. In the Map tab each map now has a Region as well as a name:

  • A map with its own name shows that name.
  • A map with no name shows its region (for example "Whisperwood").
  • A map with neither shows "near" the closest named map (up to three maps away), or "Uncharted land" when there is

none.

To give many maps a region at once, type the region in the Map tab, press Paint this region on the world map and click the maps on the world map (M). The world map shows region names, and "You enter ..." uses the region when a map has no name.

Big things in one click

The Buildings tab has a new Big things row above the building layouts: the broadleaf trees (short, normal and tall), and every multi-cell thing in the catalogue (wells, lamps, statues, stalls, carts, tents and more). Click one, then click the map to place the whole thing; R turns it and Ctrl+Z undoes it. The empty cells around a big thing no longer wipe what is already on the map.
