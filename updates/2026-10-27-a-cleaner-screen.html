A cleaner, clearer screen

2026-10-27

The things you look at all the time have been rebuilt.

  • Your character panel (top left) shows a round portrait of your character with your level on a badge. Health and

spell power are thick bars with the numbers inside them. When you are hit, a pale trail drains behind the health bar so you can see how much the blow took, and the bar pulses when your health is low. Experience sits under them, with gold, how full you are and your weapon in a line below.

  • One action bar sits at the bottom centre: your four quick slots, Run, then your Weapon Art, Second Wind and

Battle Cry, each a square slot with its own icon and its key on a key cap. Abilities show a sweep while they recharge, with the seconds left; your Weapon Art's Focus fills along its bottom edge. Empty quick slots show a + to fill.

  • Your target gets a proper frame at the top: its level on a badge, its name, how tough it is for you, and a thick

health bar with the numbers.

  • The E prompt shows the key as a gold key cap just above the action bar.
  • Windows ease in when they open and have a clearer close button. The Equipment page no longer runs off the edge of

the screen, and Settings no longer shows "Reset window positions" twice.

  • The Skills window shows a small level bar on every skill and what its next level unlocks.
  • Shopkeepers' portraits fill their frame instead of standing small in the corner.

The compact panel (Settings > Interface) still works and now also lists your gold. Reduce motion turns off the easing and the low-health pulse.
