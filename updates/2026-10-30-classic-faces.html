Rounder, friendlier faces

2026-10-30

The new faces turned out to look a little odd at the size the game draws them: angular, with eyes high under the hairline and large patches of shading. Faces are now drawn the way most games of this kind draw them at world size:

  • a round face with a soft, curved chin;
  • flat golden skin with one gentle shadow under the hair;
  • two dark oval eyes with a small highlight, set low on the face, in a dark shade of each person's eye colour;
  • no brows and no nose, and a small mouth.

This applies to your own character in every direction, while walking and fighting, under every hat, cap, crown, hood and helmet that shows the face. The face stays in the same place whatever you wear, and where a visor leaves only a narrow opening, the eyes sit inside it.

All the townsfolk and the people of Skaldmark, Soleira and Eisenwald have the same faces. Bearded men have a moustache in their beard's colour over the mouth, the mermaids keep their own peach skin, helmets keep their nasal bars and cheek pieces, and grey-haired elders keep their grey brows. Everyone still blinks, each on their own rhythm (see Characters that blink).

The hair, outfits and hands from A new face for your character, New faces and hair for the townsfolk and New faces and hair across Skaldmark, Soleira and Eisenwald are unchanged. The Original graphics setting is unchanged.
