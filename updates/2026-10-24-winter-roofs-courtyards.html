Winter roofs and lived-in courtyards

2026-10-24

The approved building pieces and props are now placed in source for Skaldmark and Soleira. The existing sleds, carved rune stones, log corners and timber fences are reused alongside them.

Skaldmark's modular turf roofs gain snow-covered versions on snowy maps. A pair of working fish racks stand by Ravnsund and Isvik's smokehouses. A small ice sheet and drifting floes sit offshore at Isvik; the fishing holes and piers remain clear, and the water keeps its usual movement and access rules.

Soleira's rooms use warm terracotta, pale clay inlays and blue azulejos. The mill keeps its timber floor. Castamar gains a short arcade, striped shop awnings, another style of iron balcony and a grapevine pergola in its gardens. An olive press stands in Corcova's yards, flowerpots dress a few steps, and a stork nests on a chimney rising from Alvarena's tiled roofs. A guitar rests in La Paella Dorada; it is a decorative prop in this batch.

The additions have been checked in fresh private worlds with both regions generated together. Nothing has been released or installed.
