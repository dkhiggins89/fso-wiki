Eisenwald horse and woodland revisions

2026-10-25

Live since 26 October 2026. This is the earlier review. All three revisions have since been approved and added to source; Eisenwald encounters and maps are still being built.

The approved Werewolf and Hunt Hound artwork has been added unchanged, and the Wild Boar has been enlarged uniformly by 1.4 times. Its tusks, pointed ears and bristly mane are retained. The larger artwork uses authored foot anchors; existing creatures keep their previous placement.

The Wild Huntsman has a revised mount with an arched neck, a longer horse head, flowing spectral mane and tail, and a small saddle cloth. The approved rider and the mist disappearance are retained. The Forest Kobold is smaller than the enlarged Boar, with its miner's hood, pickaxe and ore pouch intact. The Wildcat has shorter, sturdier legs, a low stalking body, a gentle arch to its back and softer, irregular tabby markings.

Those three revisions remain private previews, with standing views, motion sheets, animated sequences and a common-scale comparison beside the Boar and Iberian Lynx. They have not been added to the game. The preceding review is Eisenwald creatures revised for the forest.

Creature encounters, combat and taming will be connected as the realm is built. No world pack or generator changed in this batch. Nothing has been installed or published.

The completed creature approval and next farming review are recorded in Eisenwald creatures ready and farming review.
