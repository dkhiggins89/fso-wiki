Soleira's town clothes

2026-10-24

All of Soleira's new outfits are now approved and integrated in source. Guards wear steel helmets and armour, travellers and traders have broad hats, field workers have straw hats, and craftspeople wear linen aprons and shawls. The hermit keeps his grey beard, and the brothers have simple brown robes.

The complete set gives every Soleira townsperson a regional look. Each character retains their dialogue, shop and Original graphics. Townsfolk who dress alike share a model while keeping their own names and jobs.

The final refinements are approved and integrated: Father Tomas in a black cassock with a white collar; Duchess Leonor in a crimson gown with gold trim and a fitted gold circlet; a captain's hat for Soledad; and a brass handbell for Ramon. See Soleira townsfolk complete for the final update.

The source integration has been checked in fresh private worlds with both regions generated together, as well as real talk windows and graphics switches. Nothing has been released or installed.
