Step inside: every building in Astraeth has an interior

2026-10-18

Doors in Astraeth now lead somewhere. Walk onto a door to step inside, and walk out through the doorway at the bottom of the room to come back out beside it.

  • Elder Maren's Cottage in Dawnrest Hollow: the elder now waits for you inside, just west of where you wake.
  • Wren's Cottage in Old Oak Glade: Wren works among her herbs, with an Alchemy Lab by the window.
  • Brannoc's Forge at Wrenford Gate: both forge doors lead in. Brannoc stands by a working Anvil.
  • The Brook & Barrel on the market square: Rosa keeps the bar, and there are tables for weary travellers.
  • Wrenford Chapel on Chapel Lane: stained glass, pews, the font and the organ. Father Cedric is at the altar.
  • The Great Hall of Castle Rise: banners, suits of armour and the banquet tables. Sir Trianus stands beside the

throne.

  • The cottages at Wrenford Gate and Riverside Cottages are homes you can look round.

Wrenford Gate has been tidied: its second cottage, whose door used to face the edge of the map, now stands beside the first on the north side of the road, with the forge just east of them. A vegetable plot and a bench fill the old spot south of the road.
