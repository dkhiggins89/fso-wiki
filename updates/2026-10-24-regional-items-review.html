Regional instruments and vineyard art in preparation

2026-10-24

Live since 26 October 2026. All icons and grape fields were approved and integrated in source. See Songs, sagas and Soleira wine.

The next regional item batch has art candidates for a lyre, the Skaldmark Saga, the Soleira Chronicle, grape seeds, grapes and Soleira wine. Two matching field tiles show newly sown grapes and mature vines. The review compares them with the existing guitar, regional drink and field artwork.

The intended instruments will use the existing Songs activity. The books will be reusable lore reading, while grapes will follow the existing sow, water and harvest cycle. A wine recipe is planned at an existing cooking station. Those rules and the approved artwork are now integrated in source.

The existing Guitar identity and icon will be reused. Current regional dishes already provide food variety, so this batch avoids duplicate dishes. Optional player clothing is deferred because it would need a separate set of layers.

See Soleira townsfolk complete for the approved outfits and Winter roofs and lived-in courtyards for the approved building additions.
