White picket fence

2026-10-19

For world builders

The map editor's Fences, gates & hedges group has a fourth family: the White picket fence, painted pickets with pointed tops, rails behind them and capped posts. It has the same pieces as the timber fence: straights, ends, corners, T junctions, a crossing and gates opening north, south, east or west, each closed and open.

Every piece meets its neighbours exactly, including runs going up and down the map. Closed gates block the way; open gates can be walked through, and a closed and an open gate share the same posts, so they pair as a map gate.
