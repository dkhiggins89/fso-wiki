Grass shores, sandy ground and more iron gates

2026-10-19

For world builders

  • Grass shore (Coasts, shores & cliff edges): water with a grassy earth bank, foam and shallows, in every shape a

lake, pond, island or one-tile-wide stream needs, including bends, ends and junctions. Paint plain water with Auto edges for paths on and the map editor fits the shore pieces for you. Shore water cannot be walked on.

  • Sandy ground (Grass & ground): peach sand with a soft edge where it meets meadow grass, fitted the same way.
  • Iron railings now have gates opening north, east and west as well as south, each open and closed. The north gate

stands open at an angle.

A path that runs up to water keeps its grass edge on that side, and the water keeps its bank: they no longer count as the same kind of ground.
