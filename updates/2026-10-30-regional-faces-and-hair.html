New faces and hair across Skaldmark, Soleira and Eisenwald

2026-10-30

The people of Skaldmark, Soleira and Eisenwald now have the same new faces as the townsfolk and your own character: a shaped jaw, a smaller nose, golden skin on face and hands, small almond eyes in brown, hazel, green, blue-grey or grey, and softer brows. Standing still, they blink, each on their own rhythm (see Characters that blink).

Their hair falls in locks with uneven tips instead of smooth caps, in natural colours. Braids, buns, long hair and beards keep their style, and helmets, hoods, circlets, crowns and hats look as before. Jarl Sigrun, Duchess Leonor and Burgomaster Friedrich keep the faces shown when the new look was first tried out, with the new hair.

With this, every person in the world has the new look (see New faces and hair for the townsfolk and A new face for your character). The Original graphics setting is unchanged.
