Gravestones, tips for new players and a better Vault

2026-10-20

When you are knocked out

  • You keep your equipped gear and your three most valuable other items (by what shops pay for them). Your gold

stays with you.

  • Everything else goes into a gravestone where you fell. You are told where it is, in chat and with a

notification.

  • Only you can open your grave for 15 minutes. After that anyone can. Walk back, stand beside it and right click it

> Open to take your things back (Examine shows how long it has left).

  • After an hour the grave crumbles, and anything still in it is lost.
  • The rest is as before: you wake up at your death point with 1 health, and now and then a hard defeat weakens you

slightly.

These rules apply in Astraeth. Worlds still using the original First Star Online 1.6 rules keep the old way: one random item or some gold is dropped where you fall.

Tips for new players

The first time something happens (your first fight, level, full bag, low health, being knocked out, a new area, a tameable beast, picking something up) a short tip explains what you can do. Each tip appears once. Got it closes it and No more tips turns them off; turn them back on in Settings > Interface.

The Vault

Opening your Vault (in Wrenford Market) now shows:

  • your stored items as pictures with counts, one per kind of item, and how full the vault is;
  • a search box and filters: Gear, Materials, Food & potions, Other;
  • click an item to take one, Shift + click to take them all;
  • your bag underneath: click an item to store the whole stack, or Store everything to store all you are not

wearing (you always keep the one you are wearing);

  • your bank gold, with Deposit and Withdraw, when a banker is nearby.

Your own Vault now holds 100 items (it was 15). House and guild vaults, cabinets and crates keep their sizes.
