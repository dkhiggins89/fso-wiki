Characters that blink

2026-10-29

Characters close their eyes for a moment every few seconds while they stand still. Each player and each townsperson keeps their own rhythm, so a crowd in the market square does not blink all at once. Nobody blinks while walking or fighting.

Blinking comes with the new, more detailed faces. Your own character has them (see A new face for your character), and so do the townsfolk (see New faces and hair for the townsfolk) and the people of Skaldmark, Soleira and Eisenwald (see New faces and hair across Skaldmark, Soleira and Eisenwald). Until a face has its closed-eye picture, that character keeps its eyes open as before, and the Original graphics never blink.
