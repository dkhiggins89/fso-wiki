Housing districts, house styles and furniture

2026-10-27

You can now own a house in a city. Five cities each have a housing district behind a gate from the market, a street of six plots across three maps:

Every plot is marked: a waymark post at its front corner carries a plaque with the plot's name, and a plot for sale has a gold border with its price. Owned plots show their owner's name, and yours says "Your home".

Getting a house

  1. Earn a deed. Each district's Land Agent gives the quest A Place of Your Own: reach the level they ask for and

bring them logs for the builders. The reward is a house deed, good in any district.

  1. Buy a plot. Stand in front of a free plot and use your deed. The Housing window (B) opens: pick a house style

from those of the region and buy. The price comes from the gold you carry first, then from your bank.

  1. Move in. Your house is built at once, its door facing the street. One home per character.

Tax

Tax is 5% of the plot's price every week. Buying pays the first week. After that each week's tax is taken from your bank on its own, even while you are away. You can pay up to four weeks ahead in the Housing window.

If your bank cannot pay, the house is lost. The council takes the plot back, and everything in your House Vault and every piece of furniture in the house goes to your bank vault. You are told by mail, and warned the week before when your bank is running low.

Furniture

Floors and walls are free to change as before. Furniture is now made of real items: placing a chair, bed or bookcase uses one from your bag, and taking it down gives it back. Make furniture with Carpentry (equip a Saw: thirty pieces from a timber stool at level 1 to a throne at level 70), or buy it from the Furnisher in each district, who charges more than making it yourself.
