Finished walls, bridges and docks

2026-10-19

For world builders

  • Wall finishing pieces (Walls & windows): every Building kit wall style (cottage, Tudor, warm render, stone, barn,

brick) now has left and right corners, an upper-storey wall without the ground-floor trim, an eave row under the roof, and a wall on a stone plinth. They join the existing walls, windows and doors in any order.

  • Bridges (Doors, stairs & bridges): timber and stone, running east-west or north-south. Use a bank piece at each

end, middle pieces between, or the single span over a stream one tile wide.

  • Docks: plank decks on piles, with straights, corners, jetty ends out in the water and bank ends at the shore.

Bridges and docks can be walked on, and water painted beside them gets no bank.

  • Harbour props (Outdoor objects): mooring bollard, rope coil, crab traps, anchor, fish crates, buoys and a dock

ladder.
