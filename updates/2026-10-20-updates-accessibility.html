Automatic updates

When a new version is out, the title screen says so, with its size and what is new. Choose Update now: the game downloads it, checks it is exactly the file the official server vouches for, and installs it. On Windows the game then restarts by itself; on Linux and the Steam Deck it closes, and you start it again as usual.

  • Later puts it off. Sometimes an update is needed to keep playing with everyone else; then there is no Later, and

logging in asks you to update first.

  • Your character, settings and saves are not touched by an update.
  • Downloads come from the game's public download page on GitHub; the official server tells your game which file to

expect, and anything that does not match is thrown away.

Accessibility

New in Settings > Interface:

  • Colour-blind friendly colours: damage and healing numbers, and the online dot in Friends, use orange and blue

instead of red and green.

  • Captions for important sounds: a short caption at the bottom of the screen for sounds that tell you something,

such as a monster growling nearby, thunder, being hit or levelling up.

  • Reduce motion: no camera jolt when you are hit, and trees stop swaying.
  • Interface size now goes up to 150%.
