The Sea Wolves and the gear of the north

2026-10-23

Skaldmark gear

Twelve new pieces of Norse gear. Each one shows on your character when you wear it.

GearPieces
WeaponsSeax, Bearded Axe, Skaldmark Spear, Jarl's Sword
ShieldsPainted Round Shield, Huscarl Shield
ArmourWolfskin Coat, Lamellar Armour
HelmsWolfskin Hood, Spangenhelm, Huscarl Helm
BootsFur Boots

You can get it in three ways:

  • Buy it.
  • Smith Torvald in Vindholm sells the Seax, Bearded Axe, Skaldmark Spear and Painted Round Shield.
  • Merchant Hakon sells the Wolfskin Hood, Fur Boots and Wolfskin Coat.
  • Make it.
  • At an Anvil with Blacksmithing: the weapons, the Spangenhelm, the Huscarl Helm, the Huscarl Shield and Lamellar Armour.
  • With a Saw (Carpentry): the Painted Round Shield.
  • At a Loom with Tailoring: the furs.
  • Take it. The Sea Wolves carry it, and the best pieces come from their chieftain.

Each piece needs a skill level to use. The item pages list the level.

The Sea Wolves

Raiders in black and blood red have made camp at Ulfsnes, on the fjord's north shore, across the water from Ravnsund. Their longship is drawn up on the strand.

RaiderFights with
Sea Wolf ReaverBearded axe and round shield
Sea Wolf SpearmanSpear, mail and spangenhelm
Sea Wolf BerserkerAn axe in each hand
Sea Wolf ChieftainThe Jarl's Sword. He is much tougher than the rest.

Sea Wolf raids

When an invasion strikes a Skaldmark town (Vindholm, Ravnsund, Fjellby or Isvik), it is the Sea Wolves who come ashore: reavers, spearmen and berserkers. Drive them off together for the usual invasion pay. Towns elsewhere still get their usual invaders.

New quest: The Sea Wolves

Harbourmaster Bjarne in Ravnsund wants the raiders gone. He asks you to break them at Ulfsnes and defeat their chieftain. The reward is a Huscarl Shield.
