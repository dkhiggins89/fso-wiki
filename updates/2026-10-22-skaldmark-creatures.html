Creatures of the north

2026-10-22

Skaldmark, the cold northern land across the sea from Port Saltreach, will have its own wildlife and its own dangers:

CreatureWhat it is
Frost TrollA hulking blue troll of the snowfields with an icicle beard and a club crusted with ice.
DraugrA dead warrior risen from a barrow, in rusted helm and mail, with a notched sword and a split shield.
ElkA great elk with broad palm antlers. It leaves you alone unless you go for it.
Snow OwlA big white owl that hops through the snow and flings its wings open to strike.
Sea EagleA white-tailed eagle that hunts over the fjords, always on the wing, swooping in talons first.
Fjord WyrmThe great sea serpent of the fjord, rearing from its coils. Skaldmark's boss.

Every one of them moves in all four directions: they stand, walk, attack, flinch when hit and fall when beaten.

Elks give hides, owls and eagles give feathers, and the Fjord Wyrm leaves dragon scales for the smiths.

Also fixed

A roaming rare creature no longer gets a name like "Frost Frost Troll": a rare is never given the word its name already starts with.
