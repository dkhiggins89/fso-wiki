Moving links and quicker house interiors

2026-10-18

For world builders

Move a link. Right click a link in the map editor and choose Move this link, then click the tile where it should go on the same map. It keeps where it leads, and the way back on the other side is changed so players arrive beside the new tile. Esc cancels, and Ctrl+Z undoes the move.

House interiors from Create and go there. If you make an area with a House interior style using Create and go there, the room's doorway now starts a door link for you. Go back to the house and click its door with the Link tool: the door and the room are joined both ways. See Building a house interior.
