Geese on the Wolfbach farms

2026-10-25

Big white farm geese now wander the farms of the Wolfbach Valley in Eisenwald, and two keep watch over the Hohenburg gardens. They waddle about with their necks swaying, and if you get too close they stretch out their necks, open their wings and come at you hissing.

  • A beaten goose can drop a Raw Goose and a Feather. Roast the goose with potatoes and herbs at a stove or

campfire for a Roast Goose, or buy a Raw Goose from Butcher Gustav in Hohenburg.

  • Geese can be tamed with a Monster Treat. Kept at the ranch, a goose lays Eggs. See Beasts and taming.

With this, Eisenwald is complete: 208 maps, four towns with their own banners and townsfolk, six creatures and the Wild Huntsman, the farms and kitchen, the Brewers' Fair, and mist in the forests at night. See its region page.
