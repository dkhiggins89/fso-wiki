Hochalden's first look

2026-10-26

Not yet live: this is an art proposal awaiting approval. Nothing from this review is in the game.

The next realm is Hochalden, in the mountains south of Eisenwald. Its first review pairs a new Alpine chalet with the approved Eisenwald townhouse to compare their scale and style.

The chalet has a stone ground floor, a timber upper storey, a carved balcony, flower boxes and a broad shingle roof weighted with stones. A short covered bridge explores the same timber and stone construction.

Three red flags carry a white edelweiss with a gold First Star at its heart. The choices use an open red field, a fine gold edge, or two small gold peaks below the flower. The building direction and flag choice need approval before the next art batch begins.

The gold-edge flag B is now approved. The chalet's direction is accepted with two corrections, and the bridge is being redesigned. See Hochalden chalet and river bridge revision for the current review.
