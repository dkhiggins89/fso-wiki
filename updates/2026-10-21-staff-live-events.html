Staff can run live events

2026-10-21

Besides happening on their own, the world events can now be started by the server's staff whenever they like:

  • a Starfall on a chosen map,
  • an invasion of a chosen town,
  • Marisol the Wayfarer's caravan,
  • a roaming rare of a chosen monster and colour.

Staff can also run a festival outside its usual dates (or pause festivals), and adjust things like how often events come round, how long they last and what they pay. So keep an eye on the chat for special event days.

The numbers given on the other update pages (for example 40 Chunks of Star in a fragment, or 40 gold per invader) are the normal settings; a server may change them.
