Notifications, what's new and your own keys

2026-10-20

Notifications

Things you should not miss now pop up at the top of the screen with a soft chime, as well as appearing in the chat log:

  • new mail
  • a private message (/msg)
  • an invitation to a party or a guild
  • someone who wants to trade with you
  • a beast battle challenge

Up to three show at once and each fades after a few seconds. Click one to open the window that deals with it (Mail, Party or Beasts). When the game is in the background, its taskbar button flashes. Both can be turned off in Settings > Interface.

What's new and the wiki in the game

  • The title screen shows What's new: the latest updates from this wiki. Click one to read it.
  • Right click a monster, a person or an item on the ground and choose Open in wiki to read its page.
  • The wiki comes with the game (a wiki folder beside it), so this works without the internet; pages open in your web

browser.

Change your keys

Settings > Controls lists the keys for walking, attacking, talking, guarding and every window (Inventory, Character, Skills, Mail, Quests, Party, Beasts, Housing, World map, Chat, Help). Click a key and press the one you want. If another action already uses that key, the two swap. Reset keys to defaults puts them back. The same tab has a slider for how fast a controller's stick moves the pointer.

The arrow keys always walk, and Esc and the other F keys stay as they are.
