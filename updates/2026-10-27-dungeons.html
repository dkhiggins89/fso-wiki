Four dungeons, and where the great bosses went

2026-10-27

The great bosses of the realms have gone underground. Each region beyond Wrenford now has a dungeon of four floors: winding caves full of the region's toughest monsters, an elite on the third floor, a new guardian on the second, and the great boss waiting in the deepest hall.
