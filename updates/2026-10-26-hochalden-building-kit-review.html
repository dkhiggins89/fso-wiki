Hochalden's building kit is ready for review

2026-10-26

Not yet live: these are art candidates awaiting approval. No Hochalden art or maps have been added to the game.

Hochalden's first look is approved: the stone-based timber chalet, the gold-edged edelweiss flag and both grid-aligned covered bridges. Those approved designs are being kept unchanged.

The next review covers the realm's building kit. Joinable timber and stone walls, whitewashed bases, carved balconies, shingle roofs and gables support the Alpine towns. The landmark candidates are an onion-domed church, a town hall with a clock gable, a mountain inn, a hay barn, a dairy, a small pass chapel and a crystal-cave entrance.

Woodpiles, milk churns, cheese wheels, hay racks, wayside shrines, signposts and a stone fountain provide village and pasture details. New rope bridges run straight along either axis of the map grid, at the existing stone bridge's deck width.

Review sheets show the buildings at a common scale beside the approved chalet, the modular pieces with joined repeat samples, the props, and both rope-bridge directions. These new candidates need art approval before they are added to the game.
