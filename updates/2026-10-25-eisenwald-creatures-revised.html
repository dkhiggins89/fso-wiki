Eisenwald creatures revised for the forest

2026-10-25

Not yet live: this is the earlier revision review. The Werewolf, Hunt Hound and enlarged Boar have since been approved for source; the horse, Kobold and Wildcat have another review pass.

The Eisenwald creature designs have had another pass. The new review sheets show the previous and revised figures at the same game scale, with existing regional creatures beside them for comparison.

The Wild Boar has solid pointed ears and a denser, irregular bristly mane. The Wildcat has a rounder face, a short muzzle, tufted ears, a sleek body and thin tabby markings painted onto its coat. The Forest Kobold keeps its leather miner's hood, pickaxe and ore pouch, at a larger size.

The Werewolf leans forward with longer arms, a tapered muzzle and a ragged grey fur outline. Continuous curved skin replaces the exposed round joints across the set. The Hunt Hound has pointed ears, tattered spectral fur and a faint teal glow.

The Wild Huntsman is much larger. His KO now dissolves both rider and horse into curling mist, with no figure left in the final pose.

All six creatures remain private art candidates. Combat, taming and encounters will follow approval and client checks; no monster rules, playable maps or approved creature artwork changed in this batch. Nothing has been installed or published.

The latest approval and revision status is recorded in Eisenwald horse and woodland revisions.
