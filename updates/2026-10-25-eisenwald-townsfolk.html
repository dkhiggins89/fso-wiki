The townsfolk of Eisenwald

2026-10-25

All 35 people of Eisenwald now have their own looks, made for the realm:

  • The guards of Hohenburg wear steel kettle hats and black doublets with red and gold puffed, slashed sleeves, and

carry spears.

  • Countess Adelheid wears a red gown with a laced front and a black cloak with a white fur collar.
  • Burgomaster Friedrich wears a black robe, a flat beret and the town's gold chain of office.
  • Town Crier Emil is in red and gold, with a hand bell.
  • The baker, the brewmaster and the assayer wear white linen caps and laced bodices.
  • The butcher, the smith, the smelter and the innkeeper wear their work aprons. The innkeeper and the brewers

carry ale tankards, and the smelter carries a pick.

  • The forester and the hunter wear green felt hats with feathers. The hunter carries a hunting horn.
  • The mine foreman wears the black miner's hood and the leather worn behind, and carries a lantern.
  • The charcoal burner wears a sooty hood.
  • The chapel sisters wear white wimples and black veils, and the priest and deacon wear their robes and

stoles.

The coachmen share one look, as do the guards and the chapel sisters. With Original graphics everyone keeps their classic sprite.
