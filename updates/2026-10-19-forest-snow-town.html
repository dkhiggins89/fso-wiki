Forest grass and snow patches, new shop signs and lamps

2026-10-19

For world builders

  • Forest grass patch and Snow patch (Grass & ground): dark forest grass or snow meeting meadow grass with a soft

edge, in every shape. Paint plain forest grass or snow with Auto edges for paths on and the edge is fitted for you.

  • Shop signs (Shops & signs): tailor, butcher, fletcher, stable and ranch, on the same wall bracket as the bakery and

inn signs.

  • Lamps (Lights & decorations): a wall lantern, a garden lantern and a two-tile street lamp. All three glow at

night.
