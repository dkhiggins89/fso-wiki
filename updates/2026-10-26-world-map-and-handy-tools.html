A world map that guides you, and a dozen handy tools

2026-10-26

With the realms growing and monsters now having levels, it should always be clear where to go next and why a fight went wrong. This update adds the tools for that.

The world map (M)

  • Every place is shaded by how its monsters suit your level: grey far below you, green easy, gold about right,

orange tough, red deadly. Point at a place to see its name and level.

  • Each region shows its band ("Eisenwald · levels 12-24"), and the corner names the regions that suit you now.
  • Markers show the bosses and their levels, the starstones you know (blue stars) and your current quest goal.
  • Return to a starstone: once every 15 minutes, out of a fight, the map's button takes you back to the last starstone

you touched or travelled to.

  • Untick Show levels for the plain map.

In a fight

  • Tab attacks the nearest monster; press it again for the next one. Farm animals are only picked when nothing else

is there.

  • Quick slots (F5 to F8), beside the ability bar: click an empty one and pick food or a potion from your bag; press

the key to eat or drink without opening the bag. Right click a slot to change it. They are kept for each character.

  • Run with R or the Run button: your steps are a third quicker while your run energy lasts. Energy falls a point

for each step run and comes back as you walk, twice as fast when you rest after a fight.

  • Death recap: if you are knocked out, a window shows what hit you, its level, each of the last blows and a hint,

for example that it was several levels above you.

Getting stronger

  • Level-up summary: a new level shows your new health and how much harder you hit; a skill's new level lists what it

unlocks (weapons, armour, spells, recipes), straight from the skill guide.

  • Experience tracker: under your experience, how much you are gaining an hour and how many more of your last kill

take you to the next level.

  • Gear comparison: an item's tooltip compares it with what you wear in that slot: a weapon's average blow and the

damage armour takes off each hit, in green when better and red when worse.

Loot and your bag

  • Hold Alt to see the names of items on the ground (or tick Settings > Interface "Always show the names of items on

the ground").

  • Take all nearby: right click an item when several lie close together to pick up everything around you at once.
  • Tidy in the backpack sorts your bag: what you wear, weapons and armour, tools, food, then the rest.
  • The minimap's monster dots now use the same colours as the threat ratings, so you can see what is safe at a glance.

All new keys can be changed in Settings > Controls.
