Hochalden bridges follow the map grid

2026-10-26

Not yet live: the chalet, flag and both covered bridge orientations are approved; none has been integrated yet. No Hochalden art or maps are in the game.

Hochalden's chalet is approved as shown, and its gold-edged edelweiss flag is being kept unchanged.

The covered bridge now follows the map grid in two directions. One runs east–west over a north–south river, with its windowed side facing the viewer. The other runs north–south up the screen, with the open gabled portal at its bottom end. Both are short crossings with masonry piers standing in the water and approaches matching the existing stone bridge.

The review sheet compares both versions with the existing stone-bridge tiles at the same scale. The canopy and walkable deck are prepared as separate art layers so the crossing can sit above and below players when it is added to the game.

Both versions are now approved, completing the first-look batch. Work has continued to the full building kit, whose new art will be reviewed before integration.
