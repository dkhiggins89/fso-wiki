A gilded glass interface

2026-10-27

The interface has a new look, painted as a proper art kit: dark iron frames with a thin gold inner line, over smoked glass that shows the world behind it, softly blurred and tinted warm brown. Every panel and window uses it, so the screen feels part of the world instead of sitting on top of it.

  • Your character panel shows your head and shoulders in a gilded ring, with your level on a gold badge, and

thick glossy health and spell power bars with the numbers inside. Your experience has moved to the action bar.

  • Effects you are under appear as round icons just below it: gold-rimmed when they help (Well fed, Battle Cry,

Resting, your spells) and red-rimmed when they hurt (Hungry, Held). Each shows the time left and a sweep as it runs down; point at one for its name.

  • The target frame shows the monster in a round frame, its level on a gold badge, how tough it is for you and its

health. When it winds up a blow, a red Winding up! bar fills under its health: face it and Guard, or step away before it fills.

  • The action bar has a gold experience bar along its top with a mark every tenth of a level; point at it to see

your level and how far along you are.

  • The minimap is round, centred on you, in a studded iron ring, with the place's name on a small plate under it.
  • Your tracked quest sits under the minimap: its title, what to do next and how many you have so far.
  • Damage numbers are gold lettering that pops up over what you hit: bigger and orange for a critical hit, rose

for blows you take, teal for your companion's.

  • Monsters' health bars are small gilded bars over their heads.
  • Chat tabs are plain words; the one you are reading is gold and underlined.
  • Windows have heavier frames with gold corner pieces, and their bag slots are half see-through.
  • The Skills window (K) is redesigned. Your total level and highest skill sit at the top; the categories run

down the left with their totals; the chosen category's skills show as tiles, each with a picture, its level on a gold badge, how far you are towards its next unlock and what that unlock is. Click a skill to see what it covers and every unlock in level order: reached ones ticked, the next one lit, the rest locked. Skills rise as you use them, so the bars show progress between unlocks rather than experience.

Prefer the earlier look? The interface style setting in Settings > Graphics still offers the storybook style and the others; it takes effect the next time the game starts.
