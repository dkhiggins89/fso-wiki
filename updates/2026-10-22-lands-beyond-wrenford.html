The lands beyond Wrenford

2026-10-22

Astraeth has grown. From the Crossroads east of Wrenford, the King's Road now runs on into a whole new country: about a hundred new places to explore, joined up on foot.

Where to go

  • Eastmarch and Millbrook: follow the King's Road east from the Crossroads. Fields, orchards and hedgerows lead to

the village of Millbrook, with the Plough & Lantern inn, Garrick's forge, a market green and the farms to the north.

  • Highmoor and the Ninestones: the heather moor north of Wrenford, with an old stone circle that the shepherds

don't quite trust.

  • Stonecrest Hills and the mine: north of Millbrook, rocky hills full of ore to mine. Take the steps down into the

Stonecrest Mine, but mind the dwarves who dug there first.

  • Frostpeak Heights: further north, past Stonecrest Pass, the snowfields where the river Greyrun begins. A hermit

keeps a shrine among glowing crystals. Yetis and worse roam the snow.

  • The Greywood: a great forest east of Millbrook, cut by the Greyrun. Find the Woodcutters' Camp, the ancient

tree of Elderglade, the orc camp in Orc Hollow and the haunted Greywood Ruins.

  • The Fenmire: south of the Crossroads, a marsh of bog pools, boardwalks and drifting lights. Mother Nettle lives

in a hut on the fen, and the dead do not rest at the Drowned Chapel.

  • Mirrowmere: a broad lake with the fishing village of Mirrowford on its north shore and a little island

shrine in the middle.

  • The Saltreach Coast: beaches, dunes and the Gull Point lighthouse, and the harbour town of

Port Saltreach, with its quay, jetties, ships, the Drowned Anchor inn and a chandlery.

Many of the new places have no name of their own. Instead they show their region (Eastmarch, Greywood, Fenmire...) as you walk in.

New people and quests

Shopkeepers in the villages sell food, tools, weapons, potions and fishing gear, and there are four new quests:

  • Wolves at the Hen House: Farmer Odo at Millbrook Farms needs the wolves thinned out.
  • Trouble in the Hollow: Ranger Sylas in Elderglade wants the orcs and their lord driven out of Orc Hollow.
  • The Drowned Dead: Mother Nettle asks you to lay the restless dead of the Fenmire to rest.
  • Word for the Captain: Harbourmaster Quill in Port Saltreach has a message for Captain Morwen on the quay.

The further you go from Wrenford, the tougher the monsters get. Wolves and slimes roam the farmland, while orcs, gazers and dark fairies haunt the deep Greywood. Zombies and ghouls lurk in the fen, and yetis and snow demons stalk the Frostpeak snows.
