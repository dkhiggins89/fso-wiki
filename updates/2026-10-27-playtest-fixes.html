Longer fights, fairer taming and a home of your own

2026-10-27

These changes come from playing a new character from the start.

Fighting and levels

  • Fights last longer, both ways. A fair fight against a monster of your level now takes a dozen or more of your blows

instead of a handful, and the monster needs as many of its own to beat you, so there is time to eat, guard or step away. Monsters of every level got the same treatment, so the colour ratings still mean what they did.

  • Twice the experience a kill. Longer fights would otherwise make levelling slow.
  • The boss bar only appears when you are near a boss (or fighting it), and never covers an open skill window.

Taming

  • Taming is much fairer. A new tamer now has about a third of a chance with an unhurt Slime, more once it is hurt,

and most beasts no longer need to be nearly dead first. Tougher beasts still need more Taming.

  • Monster Treats fit in a quick slot (F5-F8): press the key next to a beast to offer it a treat.

Getting home

  • Set your home here: right click a town fountain, a well or a starstone. When you are knocked out you wake up

there instead of at the very start of the game.

  • An arrow to your grave: after a knock-out a gold arrow circles your character, pointing to your grave and naming

where it is, until you reach it.

  • A tidier knock-out window, to the side so it never covers you: who hit you, how hard, and one tip.

Skills and items

  • Skills tell you what you got: every fish, ore, log, dish or crafted item pops up as "+1 ..." above you and in chat.
  • Item tooltips explain what things are for, in shops and your bag alike: what an item makes or is made from, which

monsters drop it, what a seed grows into, which fishing spot needs it as bait.

  • The experience line says how long until your next level ("level 7 in about 9 min").
  • Fishing looks right: your character holds the rod out over the water, the line runs from its tip to a small red

and white float that dips when a fish bites.

  • Shores flow: the moving water now carries on into the shore pieces instead of stopping at the bank. (Fixed

since: on shores turned to face another way, the moving water was drawn on the wrong side, as blue strips on the grass.)
