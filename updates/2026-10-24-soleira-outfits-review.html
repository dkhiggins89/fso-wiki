Outfits for Soleira's townsfolk in preparation

2026-10-24

Live since 26 October 2026. All outfits and the four requested refinements are approved and integrated in source. See Soleira townsfolk complete.

Soleira's people have a new set of outfit candidates: broad hats for travellers and traders, straw hats for field and harbour work, linen aprons and shoulder shawls, simple clerical robes and open-faced helmets for the town guards. The Duchess and mayor have their own colours and dress details.

There are 26 shared looks for all 35 townsfolk. They use the existing FSO body, face and rig, with four native views per look. These are new interpretations of their jobs and regional setting; the review sheet compares them with the models the characters currently borrow.

The candidates have been viewed in a private copy of the game, including the talk windows, shared appearances and the switch back to Original graphics. Their dialogue, shops and Original sprites are retained. All accepted outfits and the four revised looks are now integrated in source.
