Bounties, daily and weekly challenges, rare monsters and wayside encounters

2026-10-21

Press U and open the new Bounties tab to see your bounty, your Bounty Marks, the day's and week's challenges and what your Marks can buy.

Sergeant Holt's bounty board

Right click Sergeant Holt on the Dawnrest Training Green and choose Take a bounty. He posts one bounty at a time:

  • Defeat a number of one kind of monster that lives in the Vale and suits your level, or
  • Tame one or two beasts of a kind found here.

Your progress is shown in chat as you go. When the bounty is done you get Bounty Marks straight away: more for tougher monsters, and 20 extra Marks every 10th bounty and 60 extra every 50th. Then go back to Holt for another.

Do not like your bounty? Ask Holt to drop it for 15 Marks (this resets your count of bounties in a row).

Ten finished bounties earn the title "the Dutiful", fifty earn "the Relentless" (wear them from the Titles tab).

Daily and weekly challenges

Every day there are three daily challenges and every week three weekly challenges. They are the same for everyone, mixing fighting, gathering, beasts, Weapon Arts and Holt's bounties; the weekly ones can also ask you to defeat a boss, a roaming rare monster or to complete a star chart hunt.

  • Each daily challenge pays 40 gold and 3 Bounty Marks; finishing all three adds 50 gold and 10 Marks.
  • Each weekly challenge pays 200 gold and 15 Marks; all three add 300 gold and 40 Marks.

You are paid the moment a challenge is done. New daily challenges arrive at midnight (UTC) and new weekly ones on Monday; the Bounties tab shows how long is left.

Roaming rare monsters

Every so often a rare, coloured version of a Vale monster appears: a Golden Wolf, a Frost Slime, a Shadow Poison Frog... Everyone online is told where it was seen. It is much tougher than usual and only stays for ten minutes. Whoever defeats it gets a pouch of gold and often an uncut gem or Monster Treats, and everyone hears who did it.

Wayside encounters

While you are out fighting, gathering or taming, small encounters sometimes happen:

  • A lucky find: gold or a gem glinting on the ground beside you.
  • An ambush: a few of the area's own monsters leap out at you.
  • A kind traveller: your health and Focus are restored.
  • A lost beast: a hungry wild beast wanders up, weak enough to tame (it wanders off again after a few minutes).

Encounters only find players who are busy outdoors, so nothing happens while you stand idle or are indoors.

These rules apply in Astraeth and other worlds with the new skills; older worlds are unchanged.
