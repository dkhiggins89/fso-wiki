Furniture that stands on the floor

2026-10-18

Inside buildings the game used to light rooms as if they were outdoors, so at dawn and dusk furniture cast long sideways sun shadows across the floor and seemed to hover. Rooms are now lit as rooms: a steady warm light at any hour, and no sun shadows indoors.

Everything that stands on the ground (furniture, barrels, fences, wells, lamp posts, tree trunks, building fronts) now rests in a soft shadow underneath it: mostly hidden by the object, darker in the gaps such as under a table between its legs, with just a little showing round the base. Outdoors the sun shadows still fall from these as before. Your own character's shadow now sits right under their feet instead of a little below them.

Rooms also stay quiet inside: no wind, rain sounds or fireflies indoors.
