New faces and hair for the townsfolk

2026-10-30

The townsfolk now share the new face your character has: a shaped jaw, a smaller nose, golden skin on face and hands, and small almond eyes. Each person has their own eye colour (brown, hazel, green, blue-grey or grey) and the eyes sit a little differently from one person to the next, so a crowd does not look cloned. Standing still, they blink, each on their own rhythm (see Characters that blink).

Their hair is new: instead of the old smooth olive-green caps it falls in locks with uneven tips, in natural colours: browns, black, auburn, blond and some grey. Hats, helmets, clothes and the things they carry are as before. The mermaids keep their peach skin and their blue or golden hair.

The people of Skaldmark, Soleira and Eisenwald have them too (see New faces and hair across Skaldmark, Soleira and Eisenwald). The Original graphics setting is unchanged.
