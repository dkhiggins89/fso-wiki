Vendors in every city

2026-10-27

Every town now has its traders. The five big markets (Wrenford, Port Saltreach, Hohenburg, Castamar and Vindholm) each have an Armourer, a Weaponsmith, a Toolmaker, a Grocer and an Apothecary; the smaller towns have three of them.

  • What they sell suits the region. Wrenford's weaponsmith has daggers and short swords; Vindholm's has blades for

veterans of the fells. Skaldmark's traders also carry its Norse gear: seaxes, bearded axes, huscarl helms and lamellar.

  • They charge a premium. Gear costs half as much again as it is worth, so smithing your own is always cheaper;

buying is for when you need it now. Vendors buy gear back at under a third of its worth.

  • Unique rewards are never for sale: boss drops, Deeds rewards and the rarest gear still have to be earned.

Each vendor's stock is listed on their page in this wiki.
