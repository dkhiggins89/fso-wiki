Grounded shadows, evenly shaded trees and one-click house interiors

2026-10-18

At night, and wherever a shadow fell across them, the leafy tops of trees and the upper half of bushes stayed fully bright while the rest darkened, leaving a hard line across the plant. Night light, lamplight and shadows now cover the whole tree or bush.

Characters, monsters, people, dropped items and objects such as the anvil also get their soft shadow on the ground again with Dynamic lighting, so they stand on the ground instead of seeming to float.

For world builders

New House interior styles in the map editor: clicking a door with the Link tool makes a furnished room and links it both ways in one go. See Building a house interior.
