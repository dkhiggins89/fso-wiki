Town banners for six towns

2026-10-24

Fjellby now has its golden elk on green, and Isvik its pale ice star on blue. Soleira's towns have their own colours and emblems: Castamar's sun over a tower, Corcova's cork oak, Alvarena's stork and Puerto Dorado's anchor beneath the sun.

These approved designs have pole banners in the towns, hanging banners in local rooms and small pennants on stalls or shop fronts. Their cloth, emblems and colours are unchanged from the approved review.

Vindholm's raven, Ravnsund's wolf and the Sea Wolves' wolf skull have since passed their separate art review and joined these designs in source. See Ravens, wolves and raider flags for their placements.
