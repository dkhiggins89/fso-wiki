Classic faces for the ninja, the officer and friends

2026-10-31

The last old faces in the game are gone. Oraku, the Officer, the Warrior Faction Member, the Mage Faction Member, the Adventurer Faction Member, the Stripper, the Somewhat Naked Stripper and the Naked Stripper are built like your own character, and until now they still had the old big round white eyes and lemon-yellow skin.

They now match everyone else (see Rounder, friendlier faces):

  • golden skin on face, hands, arms and legs;
  • a flat round face with one soft shadow;
  • two dark eyes with a small highlight and a small mouth.

This holds while they stand, walk, attack, flinch and fall. Oraku's mask, the Officer's visor and the Warrior's helmet look as before, with the new eyes inside. Hair, clothes and armour are unchanged.
