A real quest log, and quests that branch

2026-10-27

The quest log (O)

  • Three tabs: Active, Available and Completed, each grouped by region and sorted easiest first. Every quest shows

its kind (story, region, side quest or daily) and the level it suits.

  • Click a quest to see who gave it and where, what to do now, its objectives with progress, the reward, where to

hand it in, and its own journal: what happened, step by step, and what you chose.

  • Track one quest: the tracker on the left shows just that one (with a count of the rest) and the map arrow

points at its next goal. Abandon gives a quest up; nothing is taken.

  • Locked quests sit at the bottom of the Available tab with what they need first.
  • The world map marks every person with a quest for you with a "!", coloured by how well its level suits you.

Quests in stages, with choices

Many of the new quests unfold in steps: finish one and the next begins by itself, with a new line in the journal and on the tracker. Some steps end in a choice, made in conversation. What you choose decides how the quest ends (who you return to, and what you are given) and is remembered, so later quests can open or close because of it. In the Vale Lands, where you leave the star shard decides which "Heart of the Fallen Star" you are offered.

New kinds of task:

  • Bring things to someone: carry them and talk to them.
  • Gather with a skill: fish, ore, logs or anything you make. Things already in your bag do not count.
  • Reach a level, as a goal along the way.

Daily quests

Daily quests come back every day (UTC). Guards, watchmen and fishers in every region have one: slimes on Wrenford's roads, the Millbrook watch, fresh fish at Mirrowford, Saltreach's shore patrol, Hohenburg's wildcats, Soleira's vultures and Vindholm's trolls.
