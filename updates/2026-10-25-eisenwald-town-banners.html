The banners of Eisenwald's towns

2026-10-25

Each of Eisenwald's four towns now has its own banner, as a flag on a pole, a hanging inside its main hall and a pennant over its market stalls:

  • Hohenburg: a white castle tower under a gold star, on red. It flies in the market square and at the south gate,

and over the bread and ale stalls.

  • Tannheim: a green fir on linen, for the woodcarvers' forest.
  • Brauheim: a golden hop cone over a wavy river, on blue. It also hangs over the town's ale stall.
  • Kesselbach: a silver hammer and pick crossed, on black, by the mine.

The realm's own banner, the black eagle holding the white star, still flies at the castle, the gates and the border. See Regions for every region's heraldry.
