Eisenwald creatures ready and farming review

2026-10-25

Live since 26 October 2026. This is the earlier farming review. Ten icons have since been approved and added to source; Raw Goose and the hop/potato fields have another review pass.

All six Eisenwald creature designs are now approved and in source. The Wild Huntsman keeps his cloaked rider and mist disappearance, mounted on the revised ghostly horse. The Forest Kobold keeps its smaller miner's figure, helmet and pick; the Wildcat keeps its low stalking pose, tufted ears and soft tabby coat. Their approved artwork has been added unchanged beside the Boar, Werewolf and Hunt Hound.

The creature definitions include combat, loot and material drops. The Wild Boar and Wildcat can be captured with Taming and kept as companions or at the ranch with Ranching. Existing items supply the drops, including hide, meat and silver ore. These rules are ready for worlds that add the realm; the future map and quest work will connect nighttime Huntsman encounters and his hounds.

The next art review covers hops, potatoes and cabbages, plus sausages, pretzels, sauerkraut and roast goose. Eleven new item icons and four new hop and potato field stages remain private previews. Cabbage growing reuses the existing seedling and cabbage field art. Raw goose is planned as butcher stock; the proposed dishes use existing cooking stations. No new farming art or rules have been added yet.

Ale already exists, so its art and identity will be reused for a proposed barley-and-hops recipe. Silver already has veins, ore, smelting and Silver Bar uses in Mining and Blacksmithing; Kesselbach's richer mine will use that existing system when the maps are built.

The previous review is Eisenwald horse and woodland revisions. Nothing has been installed or published, and no regional generator or authored world pack changed in this batch.
