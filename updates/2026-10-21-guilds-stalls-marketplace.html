Guild ranks and vaults, guild halls, market stalls and the Marketplace

2026-10-21

Guilds

Press G (or the crest in the bar at the bottom) to open the Guild window. If you are not in a guild you can start one there (a name and a 2-4 letter tag); the old chat commands (/guild, /invite, ACCEPT, /g) still work.

Ranks

RankCan
LeaderEverything: ranks, the tag and crest, the treasury
OfficerInvite, promote and remove Members and Recruits, set the message of the day, use the treasury
MemberTake items out of the Guild Vault
RecruitPut items into the Guild Vault (but not take them)

New members join as Members. The tag shows before every member's name, for example "[STR] Rowan", and the Leader chooses a crest (an emblem and a colour) and can post a message of the day.

The Guild Vault holds shared items and a treasury of gold. Open it on the Vault tab while standing beside Lir the Merchant in Wrenford Market, at a banker, or in your guild hall. Everything going in and out, and every rank change, is written in the guild Log.

Guild halls: any member can turn their house into the guild hall. In Housing (B) choose Welcome my guild (guild hall): members of your guild may then come in and use the Guild Vault there, while everyone else stays outside.

Market stalls

Talk to Lir the Merchant in Wrenford Market and choose Market stall to rent a stall in the square: 100 gold for 7 days (one each). Stand at your stall (it has your name over it) to put things out from your bag at your own prices, up to eight listings. Other players right click your stall and choose Browse to buy, even while you are offline. Takings wait in the stall's till: collect them at your stall or from Lir. When the rent runs out, anything unsold goes to your Vault and the takings to your bank; renew any time from Lir.

The Marketplace

Right click Lir and choose Marketplace to trade with everyone through orders:

  • Sell order: the items you offer are held, and sell to anyone who wants them at your price or more.
  • Buy order: the gold is held, and you buy from anyone selling at your price or less (if you offered more than the

seller asked, you get the difference back).

  • Trades happen automatically at the price of the order that was waiting first. Sellers pay a 2% fee.
  • What you bought or earned waits in your collection box on the My orders tab; collect it from Lir. You can cancel

an order there too, and everything held comes back to the box.

  • Look up any item to see its cheapest offer, best bid and recent prices. You can have up to ten open orders.

These rules apply in Astraeth and other worlds with the new skills; older worlds keep the 1.6 guilds. Stall rent, stall days, the Marketplace fee and the Guild Vault's size are the normal settings; a server may change them.
