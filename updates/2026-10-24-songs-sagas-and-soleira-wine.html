Songs, sagas and Soleira wine

2026-10-24

Not yet live: this arrives with the next release.

Lyre and the existing Guitar now open the Songs activity when used from inventory. Play the existing note phrases to benefit nearby allies. Instruments are kept after playing, and the skill-menu activity remains available. Both instruments can also be made with Carpentry and a Saw.

The Skaldmark Saga tells of Vindholm's first hearth and the northern towns' oaths. The Soleira Chronicle records roads, roofs, cork carts and returning ships. Use either book to open a reading window; each book is kept, with no spell learned or experience awarded for rereading.

Grape Seeds grow into Grapes through the existing Farming activity. Prepare soil, sow, water if desired, let the crop ripen and harvest it. A small vineyard now sits beside Los Molinos. Soleira Wine is made from grapes and Cork at an existing Stove or Campfire and uses the usual food and drink rules.

Regional shops stock the new supplies: Skald Eirik has the northern instrument and saga; Rafaela has a guitar and local wine; Isidro has the chronicle; Carmen and Dolores stock grape seeds. Existing regional dishes and mead remain part of the town kitchens, so this batch adds no duplicate dishes.

All item and field art was approved before integration. The existing Guitar artwork is retained. Both regions were regenerated and checked together in fresh private worlds. Nothing has been installed or released.
