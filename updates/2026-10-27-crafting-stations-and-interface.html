New crafting stations and twenty interface improvements

2026-10-27

Crafting stations

The Anvil, Furnace, Alchemy Lab, Loom, Tanning Rack and Fletching Bench now stand in the world as real objects in the same style as the buildings around them, instead of their inventory pictures. The furnace has a chimney and a glowing fire mouth, the loom and tanning rack stand tall, and the Campfire is the lit campfire you know from the Vale. Treasure chests have a new look too: thrown open on a heap of gold and gems.

The interface

  1. Esc closes the window on top, one at a time; with nothing open it brings up the menu.
  2. The window you click or open comes to the front.
  3. Active effects (Haste, Stone skin and others) show as small chips under your bars.
  4. Low health: the edges of the screen glow red and your health turns red under a quarter.
  5. Hunger colours the gold line orange when hungry and red when very hungry, with a warning in chat.
  6. Badges on the menu icons: new mail, and quests ready to hand in.
  7. A full bag says so on the Bag icon.
  8. Click the quest tracker to open the quest log on that quest.
  9. The minimap shows a "!" over people with a quest for you and a "?" where one is ready to hand in.
  10. Each new place shows its name for a moment as you walk in.
  11. The E prompt: "E Talk to Bess", "E Use Anvil", "E Pick up 3 Apples" whenever E would do something.
  12. Right-click menus are numbered: press 1 to 9 to choose.
  13. A hand cursor over anything you can interact with.
  14. "+50" or "−20" rises beside your gold when it changes.
  15. Shops say how many you have of each thing, buying as well as selling.
  16. Experience pop-ups add up into one "+20 XP" instead of stacking.
  17. Tooltips come up quicker.
  18. Empty quick slots fade until you open your bag.
  19. F3 hides the interface for screenshots; F3 again brings it back.
  20. Reset window positions in Settings > Interface puts every window back where it first opens.
