Beaches, snowy shores, puddles and cracks

2026-10-19

For world builders

  • Sand beach shore and Snow shore (Coasts, shores & cliff edges): water meeting peach sand, with wet sand and

foam, or meeting snow, with an icy ledge. They come in every shape the grass shore does. Paint plain water with Auto edges for paths on and the editor picks the shore that matches the ground around it.

  • Puddles and cracks (Grass & ground): small, large, muddy and paired puddles, and thin, branching, paving and web

cracks. Paint them on the overlay layer over any ground; you can walk over them.
