Skills that grow with you

2026-10-18

Gear and spells have levels

Every weapon now needs a level in its fighting skill, armour, helms and shields need Defence, robes need their magic, and spells need their school. Your starting gear is all level 1, and anything you already wear stays on. Check an item's page or tooltip, or the skill guide (Esc › Skill guide), to see what it needs.

Every skill has milestones

  • Lumberjacking finds Oak, Willow, Yew and Elder logs as you level, for new bows and staves in Fletching.
  • Mining turns up sapphires, rubies, diamonds and elemental chunks; gems become rings in Blacksmithing.
  • Farming opens Flower, Rose, Premium and Magic seeds.
  • Excavation digs up Clay, Old Coins, Fossils and Buried Relics.
  • Cooking and Alchemy: better fish and rarer potions need higher levels.
  • Meditation, Musicianship, Tracking, Appraisal and Ranching gain real perks:

stronger meditation, longer and wider songs, longer-reaching tracking that names monsters, better shop prices, and a bigger ranch with a chance of twins.

Some of the new finds are placeholders for now; uses for them will follow.
