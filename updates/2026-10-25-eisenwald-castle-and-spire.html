Eisenwald castle and taller spire

2026-10-25

Hohenburg's castle now stands on a tall rocky crag. Switchback stairs climb to its gate beneath a dominant stone keep and round towers with solid battlements. The stair cells lead up from the street, while the surrounding cliff blocks movement.

The Church of the First Star has a taller tower with a slender pointed spire. This is the preferred church for Hohenburg; the earlier small version remains available as a separate building. Chunky dark wooden eave and jetty ends finish the timber kit.

All three revisions were approved before being added. The complete buildings are available through their saved layouts, with their original model shadows and walk-behind upper portions. Eisenwald's towns, rooms and quests follow in later batches. Nothing has been installed or published.
