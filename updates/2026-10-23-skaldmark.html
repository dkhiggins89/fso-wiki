Skaldmark, the realm of the north

2026-10-23

Not yet live. Skaldmark arrives with the next update.

North of the Frostpeak Shrine, a pass climbs through the mountains into Skaldmark, a cold northern realm of fjords and pine forest. It is the first of the realms beyond the Vale Lands.

Getting there

  • On foot: go north from the Frostpeak Shrine, through Skaldmark Gate and the Grenwood.
  • By ship: Captain Morwen sails from Port Saltreach to Ravnsund, and Captain Ragna sails back.
  • By coach: coaches run from Vindholm to Ravnsund, Fjellby and Isvik and back. The road to Isvik is by dog sled.
  • By starstone: each of the four towns has a starstone. Touch one to remember it, then travel between the ones you

know.

Skaldmark's creatures live here: Frost Trolls, Draugr, Elk, Snow Owls, Sea Eagles, Reindeer and Goats, and the Fjord Wyrm.
