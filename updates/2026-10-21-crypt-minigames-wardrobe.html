The Sunken Crypt, the Fishing Derby, the Vale Run and the wardrobe

2026-10-21

The Sunken Crypt

Right click Father Cedric in Wrenford Chapel and choose Enter the crypt. You go down with the members of your party who are standing close by, into a crypt of your own: nobody else can wander in.

  • The dead rise in three waves: Zombies, then Skeletons, then Ghouls. Each wave has one more of them for every

extra member of your party.

  • When the last wave falls, Death, the Crypt Warden wakes. Like the Vale's other bosses it marks the ground before

it strikes, so step out of the marked cells, and it calls helpers when it is badly hurt. It has more health for a bigger party.

  • When the Warden falls a chest appears where it stood. Each of you may open it once for gold and a chance of gems,

a Star Chart, Beast Tonics or Monster Treats. The Warden itself sometimes drops the Necromancer's Robe.

  • Walk onto the stairs at the bottom of the room to go back up. The crypt is cleared away once everyone has left,

and if you are knocked out you wake up on the surface.

  • After a visit you must rest for 10 minutes before going down again.

The line under the boss bar shows which wave you are on and how many are left. Cleared crypts count on the new Sunken Crypt board in the Hall of Fame.

The Fishing Derby

Every few hours everyone online is told that a Fishing Derby has begun. For the next 10 minutes every fish you land counts, at a fishing spot or with the Follow the ripple work. Your count and the leader are shown at the top of the screen. When the derby ends the top three anglers are named: the winner gets 300 gold, second place half of that and third a quarter. Derby wins have their own board in the Hall of Fame.

The Vale Run

Right click Sergeant Holt on the Dawnrest Training Green and choose Start the Vale Run. Run through Whisperwood Edge, Whisperwood Crossing and Wrenford Gate to Wrenford Market, in that order. Your clock and the next checkpoint are shown at the top of the screen. Your best time is kept, and the fastest runners are on the Vale Run board in the Hall of Fame.

The run ends if you are knocked out or take longer than 10 minutes. Ask Holt again to start over.

The wardrobe and dyes

Press Y (or the hanger in the bar at the bottom) to open the Wardrobe.

  • Your look: choose what others see on your head, body and feet from anything you have ever owned (the

pieces in your Codex). Your real gear keeps doing the fighting and protecting; only how you look changes.

  • Dyes: recolour your outfit, or your headwear, in one of eleven colours (Crimson, Ember, Golden, Forest, Teal,

Royal, Violet, Rose, Midnight, Coal and Snow) for 50 gold each. Skin stays as it is.

  • Wear my own gear again clears your look and your dyes.

The Robe of Invisibility cannot be worn as a look.

For the server's owner

The Live events tab in the server app has Fishing Derby now and End derby buttons, switches for the Sunken Crypt and the Fishing Derby, and settings for how often a derby comes, how long it lasts, the winner's gold and the price of a dye. Staff with Manage events can use /event derby and /event end derby in the game.

These rules apply in Astraeth and other worlds with the new skills; older worlds are unchanged.
