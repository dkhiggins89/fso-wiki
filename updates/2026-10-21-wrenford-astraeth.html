Welcome to Wrenford, in the world of Astraeth

2026-10-21

The town has a new name: Brookhaven is now Wrenford. Wrenford Gate, Wrenford Market, Wrenford Chapel and the rest of the town's places carry the new name, and so do signs, quests and what its people say.

The valley around it is Wrenford Vale, and the world it sits in now has a name of its own: Astraeth. Wrenford is only a small market town at the edge of a much bigger world, and there is a great deal more of Astraeth still to find.

Nothing else changes: your character, bag, house and progress are where you left them, and the Wrenford deeds are the old Brookhaven deeds under their new name.
