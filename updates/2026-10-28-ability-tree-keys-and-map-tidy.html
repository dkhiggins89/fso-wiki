An ability tree, click-to-walk and a map tidy

2026-10-28

The ability tree (A)

Every weapon now has three arts, unlocked by its skill at 1, 15 and 35: Z, Shift+Z and Ctrl+Z. Swap weapons and your ability bar swaps. Swords get Cleave, Riposte and Whirlwind; daggers Puncture, Evade and Backstab; axes and hammers Stunning Blow, Armour Break and Earthshaker; bows Volley, Pinning Shot and Barrage; staves Arcane Blast, Mana Shield and Chain Blast; bare hands Flurry, Disorient and Pressure Point. No class to choose. See Abilities. Second Wind and Battle Cry grow with their skills too.

Keys that make sense

  • Q opens your quests (it was O), A the Abilities, D defends (it was Q), W the Wardrobe (it was Y).
  • W A S D no longer walk. Click to walk. If you like the arrow keys, turn on Walk with the arrow keys too in Settings;

they always work aboard a vehicle. A controller still walks with its stick.

  • Settings > Controls lists every key you can change.

A cosier start

New characters now wake up inside Elder Maren's Cottage, with Maren right beside you, instead of out by the pond. Characters you already have stay where they were.

Tidier maps

  • Fences, hedges and railings that did not meet at their corners (the Ranch's hedge frame had its whole bottom row

turned the wrong way, and a few hedge ends pointed at nothing) now join up.

  • No monster starts hidden any more: 367 spawns that stood on blocked cells, under roofs or under tree crowns were moved

to open ground, and a monster that wanders behind a tree is now drawn over the crown so you can always see it.
