Levelling up feels like levelling up

2026-10-27

Reaching a new level is now a moment worth working for.

  • As you get close, the experience bar starts to glow, brighter the nearer you are.
  • When you level, a fanfare plays, the screen flashes softly, light and sparks burst from your character, and the

new level punches in on a banner with light behind it. The health and damage you gained count up in front of you.

  • Every tenth level is a milestone: a bigger fanfare and burst, and every player in the world hears about it.
  • Players near you see a burst of light over you when you level, and you see theirs.

Skill points get the same idea, a little smaller:

  • a quick chime, a small burst and "+1 Mining" (or whichever skill) rising over your head, and a note at the top of the

screen with the skill's new level;

  • several gains close together chime a little higher each time, so a good streak sounds like it is building;
  • a skill point that unlocks something glows gold, and the summary under the minimap lists what you can now do.

Reduce motion (Settings > Interface) keeps all of this but the flash, the punch-in and the flying sparks.
