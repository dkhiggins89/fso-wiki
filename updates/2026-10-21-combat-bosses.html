Weapon Arts, bosses, item tiers, sets and gems

2026-10-21

The ability bar

A new bar sits just above the icon dock with three buttons:

ButtonKeyWhat it does
Weapon ArtZYour weapon's own move. It uses half of your Focus, which refills over a minute.
Second WindXHeals a third of your health. Ready again after 90 seconds.
Battle CryV+3 attack for 12 seconds. Ready again after 60 seconds.

The keys can be changed in Settings > Controls. Each button shows how long it has left before it is ready again.

Bosses

Each region of Astraeth now has a boss. A boss stands guard off the road, and everyone on its map sees its name and health bar at the top of the screen.

BossWhereIts unique drop
Mega Slime, the Pond MotherMossy PondIce Sword
Ogre, the Bridge OgreWhisperwood Crossing, across the riverThor's Hammer
Hellhound, the Crossroads HoundCrossroadsFire Shield

How a boss fights:

  • When you come close, it gets ready to slam. The cells about to be hit glow red for two seconds: step out

of them. Anyone still standing there when the slam lands takes a heavy hit. The Bridge Ogre slams in a line in front of it; the others slam all around them.

  • At half health it enrages. Its slams come faster and it calls helpers (Slimes or Wolves).
  • When it falls, everyone gets told. You get gold, often an uncut gem, and sometimes its unique item. The

boss returns after five minutes.

Your boss kills are counted in the Codex's Bestiary.

Item tiers

Item tooltips now show a tier in its own colour: Common, Fine, Rare or Epic, depending on what the item is worth, and Unique for the bosses' drops.

Armour sets

Wear matching pieces of the same family (helm, armour, boots, shield) for a bonus:

  • 2 pieces: 1 less damage taken from every hit.
  • 3 or more pieces: 2 less damage taken and +1 attack.

The families are Leather, Chain, Plate, Ice, Fire, Gold, Rune, Pharaoh and Knight. Item tooltips say which set a piece belongs to, and the ability bar's tooltips show the set you are wearing.

Gems

Uncut gems (from mining, digging and bosses) can be set into your gear. Stand next to an Anvil, right click it and choose Set a gem, then pick the item and the gem:

GemIn a weaponIn armour
Ruby+2 attack
Sapphire1 less damage taken
Diamond+1 attack and 1 less damage taken+1 attack and 1 less damage taken

The gem is used up. An item holds one gem; setting a new one replaces (and loses) the old one. The tooltip shows the gem.

These rules apply in Astraeth and other worlds with the new skills; older worlds fight as before.
