Marshland

2026-10-19

For world builders

  • Bog water (Water & lava) with a Bog shore (Coasts, shores & cliff edges): murky green water with duckweed and

a muddy bank, in every shape. Paint bog water with Auto edges for paths on and the shore is fitted for you. Bog water cannot be walked on.

  • Mud with Mud patch edges (Grass & ground): wet mud with a soaked edge against meadow grass. You can walk on it.
  • Boardwalk (Doors, stairs & bridges): planks on posts over bog water, with straights, corners, T junctions, a

crossing and two kinds of end: a bank end that steps onto the shore and a jetty end that stops out in the water. You can walk along it, and bog painted beside it gets no bank.

  • Marsh plants and wildlife: bog cotton, marsh marigolds, a sedge tussock, a fungus stump, a sunken cart wheel, a

heron, a dead marsh tree and a will-o'-the-wisp that glows pale blue at night.
