Star chart hunts, Starfall, invasions, Marisol's caravan, festivals and clearer quests

2026-10-21

Clearer quests

  • The quest tracker under your status panel now says where to go next: "Head to Mossy Pond", or "Marked on your

map" when it is close by. It picks the next step of your top quest: the person to talk to, the place to visit, the nearest map where the monsters you need live, or whoever you hand the quest in to.

  • A gold marker bobs over the spot on the map, a gold diamond shows it on the minimap, and when it is further

away a gold arrow on the minimap's edge points the way.

  • Accepting or finishing a quest plays a short scene: letterbox bars and the quest's name in gold, and for some

quests the camera glides to show you where to go. Click or press Esc to skip it, or turn scenes off in Settings > Interface ("Short scenes when a quest starts or ends").

Star chart hunts

Star Charts sometimes drop when you defeat monsters (roaming rares and bosses drop them far more often). You can also buy one from Sergeant Holt for 40 Bounty Marks, or from Marisol's caravan. You can carry one at a time.

Use the chart in your bag to read it. Each chart is a hunt of three to five stars around the Vale:

  • Dig: "Dig 2 steps east and 1 step south of the Campfire at Wrenford Market." Stand on the spot and press **Dig

here in the chart's window; you need a Shovel**. Digging on the right map tells you whether you are warm or cold, and with Tracking 20 or more exactly how many steps away the spot is. Digging trains Excavation.

  • Show: "Show this chart to the one who keeps the forge burning." Right click that person and choose Show chart.
  • Emote: "Wave beside the signpost at Mossy Pond." Stand close to it and use the emote (for example /wave).

The last star gives you a Starbound Chest. Open it from your bag for gold and a few prizes (gems, Monster Treats, Beast Tonics, pies, Chunks of Star), and sometimes a rare outfit; everyone hears when someone finds one. Completed hunts count on the new Star charts board in the Hall of Fame, and the weekly challenges can ask you to complete one.

World events

Everyone online is told when one of these starts:

  • Starfall: every hour or two a fragment of the First Star lands somewhere outdoors. Walk up with a **Mining

Hammer, right click it and choose Mine** for Chunks of Star and Mining practice, until it is mined away or fades after half an hour.

  • Invasions: every couple of hours monsters attack one of the towns (Wrenford Market, the Gate, Riverside

Cottages or the Ranch). Drive them all off within 15 minutes and everyone who helped is paid 40 gold and 4 Bounty Marks for each invader they defeated.

  • Marisol the Wayfarer's caravan: every two or three hours her caravan stops in Wrenford Market for 20 minutes.

Right click it and choose Trade to buy rarer goods for gold: a Star Chart, uncut gems, Chunks of Star, Beast Tonics and Monster Treats, a few of each per visit.

The Bounties tab (U) lists what is happening right now.
