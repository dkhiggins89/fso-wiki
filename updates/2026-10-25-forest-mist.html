Mist in the iron forest

2026-10-25

The deep forests of Eisenwald, the fir woods of the Eisenwald and the Beechwood, now fill with mist after dark. Moonlit grey banks drift slowly between the trees through the night and turn pale in the early morning, then lift as the day brightens. By midday the forest is clear. The mist never comes with rain, and it stays out of the towns, houses and caves.

The mist stays put on the ground as you walk through it. Like the northern lights and the dust devils it is only for looks: it does not hide monsters or change anything you can do. Reduce Motion holds it still and makes it softer, and turning off HD graphics or lighting hides it. See Regional atmosphere.
