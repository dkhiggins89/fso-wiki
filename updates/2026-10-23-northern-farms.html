Northern farms

2026-10-23

Not yet live. These arrive with the next update; the reindeer and goats come with Skaldmark's maps.

New crops

Plant them on a tilled plot like any other seed:

SeedFarmingGrows into
Barley Seeds12Barley
Rye Seeds22Rye
Cloudberry Seeds30Cloudberries

Each crop has its own look in the field: pale golden barley with long whiskers, tall blue-green rye, and low cloudberry plants with amber berries. Cloudberries are good to eat straight from the plant.

New dishes

Cook them at a hearth:

  • Barley Porridge: barley and milk.
  • Rye Bread: rye.
  • Cloudberry Tart: cloudberries, rye and milk.

Reindeer and goats

Skaldmark's farms keep reindeer and goats. Both are gentle and leave you alone. You can tame them for the ranch, where reindeer give hides and goats give milk. The elk, the snow owl and the sea eagle can be tamed too.
