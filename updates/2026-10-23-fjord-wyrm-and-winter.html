The Fjord Wyrm, snowfall and the Festival of Lights

2026-10-23

The Fjord Wyrm

The Terror of the Vindfjord now fights as a boss at Wyrm's Rest. It has far more health than anything else in the north. Watch the ground: before it strikes, a line of cells in front of it glows red. Step out of the line. Sea Eagles come to its aid once it is badly hurt. If you defeat it you get a share of its hoard and a chance of a rare blade. Like the other bosses, it returns a few minutes after it falls.

Snow

Snow now falls on Skaldmark's high fells and in the frozen north. You see it outdoors only, never in caves or houses. While it snows the wind is quieter.

Music of the north

Every Skaldmark map has its own calm Nordic theme, which takes the place of the daytime music.

Fjord fishing

Watch for fjord shoals in Skaldmark's waters. Fish them with a Fishing Net or a Harpoon to catch:

  • Herring, which you can pickle;
  • Cod, which dries into Stockfish;
  • Halibut, which cooks into a Halibut Steak.

Liv the Fishwife in Ravnsund sells herring, cod and nets. There is also new food and drink:

  • Cloudberry Mead. Brew it at a hearth from cloudberries and barley, or buy it from Brewer Gerd in Vindholm.
  • Rune Charm. A ring that wards off a little damage. Smith it from silver and ice, or buy it from Runecarver Orm in Vindholm.

The Festival of Lights

Each year from 1 to 14 December, Skaldmark lights its lamps against the long night. During the festival, falling stars come twice as often. Server staff can also start or stop it from the Live events tab.

Deeds of Skaldmark

There is a new set of Deeds (Novice, Adept and Master) for exploring and fighting your way across Skaldmark. The Master tier gives a Huscarl Helm, a Rune Charm and the title "of the North". There are also two new feats:

  • Wyrmslayer, for slaying the Fjord Wyrm several times;
  • Raider-bane, for defeating many Sea Wolf Reavers.
