Log walls and turf roofs

2026-10-23

Not yet live. These tiles arrive with the next update.

Skaldmark's farms and fishing villages are built of logs and roofed with turf. Mappers can now build those houses piece by piece in the map editor, the same way as the other building kits.

Log walls

Round tarred logs laid in courses. Every piece fits beside every other piece, in any order, and the walls stack upwards for taller houses.

  • Wall, window, window with shutters and door.
  • Corners (left and right): the side wall's log ends stacked up as round cut faces.
  • Stone footing: the bottom row on fieldstones, with its own corners and its own door.
  • Under the eave: the top row, in the roof's shadow, with a carved eave board, and its corners.
  • Gable (left and right halves): upright boards under a carved bargeboard, for the end of a house.

Turf roofs

A living roof of sod and grass that stacks upwards and sideways:

  • Turf roof, and turf roof with flowers to mix in among the plain ones.
  • Eave: birch bark and the log that holds the sod, under a fringe of long grass.
  • Ridge: the grass standing up along the crest.
  • Bargeboards for the left and right edges, and a little smoke louvre.

You'll find them in the map editor under Walls & windows and Roofs & awnings.
