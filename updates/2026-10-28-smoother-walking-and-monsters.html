Smoother walking and monsters

2026-10-28

Walking. While walking you could sometimes jump back a few tiles and then forward again, or stutter for a moment. The game sent you everything about your character about ten times a second, including your whole quest log and deeds, even when nothing had changed. That was so much at once that some of it was lost on the way and had to be sent again, so the game fell seconds behind. It could not confirm your steps in time, so it moved you back to the last place it knew about, then forward again when the late news arrived. Now only what has changed is sent, which is about a fiftieth of the amount. Steps are confirmed in a tenth of a second or so, even while you click to walk.

Monsters. A monster that was mid-step when you crossed onto the next screen jumped to the end of its step and then kept its walking legs going while standing still, sometimes until it next moved. It now finishes the step from where it was. A monster chasing you also used to stop for a moment on every tile. It now walks on at its own pace until it stops.

Controller. The left shoulder button (LB) is Defend again. Since today's key changes it walked you east instead.
