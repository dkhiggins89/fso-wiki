New fences, hedges and railings

2026-10-19

For world builders

The map editor's Fences, gates & hedges group has three new families, each a complete set that joins up in any direction:

  • Timber fence: post-and-rail fencing with straights, ends, corners, T junctions, a crossing and gates opening

north, south, east or west, each closed and open.

  • Clipped hedge: the same pieces as a trimmed hedge, with timber gates set into it.
  • Iron railings: spear-tipped railings on stone posts, with a standalone post and a south-facing gate.

Every piece meets its neighbours exactly, so you can lay a whole enclosure without gaps or steps, including runs going up and down the map. Closed gates block the way; open gates can be walked through. Pair a closed and an open gate for a map gate: their posts are in exactly the same place.
