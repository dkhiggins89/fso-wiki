Elite monsters, regional wolves and busier town squares

2026-10-27

Elite monsters

Deeper in the Vale Lands, Eisenwald, Soleira and Skaldmark, some monsters are elites: an Alpha wolf, boar or eagle, an Elder slime or serpent, an Ancient skeleton or draugr, a Veteran orc or troll. You will know them by their colour and the name over their head.

  • An elite is three levels above the monsters around it, has two and a half times the health and hits harder.
  • Beating one gives three times the experience, a purse of gold, and a one in four chance of an uncut gem.
  • There are ten in the Vale Lands, fourteen each in Eisenwald and Soleira and sixteen in Skaldmark, at most one to a

map, all in the harder half of their region. They come back like any other monster.

Wolves of every realm

Each realm's wolves now wear their own coat: pale Snow wolves on the fells of Skaldmark, dark Timber wolves in Eisenwald's forests and tawny Sierra wolves in Soleira's hills. They fight like any wolf of their level and count as wolves for quests, bounties and the bestiary.

Busier town squares

Hohenburg Market, Castamar's Plaza Mayor, Vindholm Market, Millbrook and Port Saltreach have market stalls with someone selling from each (ale and pretzels, oranges and flowers, fish, cloth and wool), and townsfolk who stop to chat. Right click a stallholder to see their wares.
