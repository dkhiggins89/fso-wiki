Eisenwald - the iron forest

2026-10-25

East of the Stonecrest Hills lies Eisenwald, the iron forest: dark fir woods, a winding river valley, timber-framed towns and mining hills full of silver. It is 208 maps.

Getting there

  • On foot: walk east from the Stonecrest Hills through the gap at their eastern edge. You arrive at Grenzstein,

where Border Warden Konrad keeps watch.

  • By boat: Captain Morwen on the Port Saltreach quay sails down the strait to Brauheim, and Captain Henrike sails

back.

Coaches run from Hohenburg to Tannheim, Brauheim and Kesselbach, and back. Each of the four towns has a starstone.

The land is split into the Eisenwald (dark fir forest), the Beechwood, the Wolfbach Valley (farms and hop gardens), the Sun Slopes (vineyards), the Kessel Hills and the Grey Coast. Eisenwald has its own music, a bright folk tune.

Creatures

  • Wild Boar: roots through the Beechwood and the valley farms. It can be tamed.
  • Wildcat: stalks the forests. It can be tamed.
  • Forest Kobold: small mischievous silver thieves in the Kessel Hills and the Kesselbach mine.
  • Werewolf: prowls the deep forest and the Wolf Tower.
  • Hunt Hound: a spectral hound that runs with the Wild Hunt.
  • Wild Huntsman: a ghostly rider and the realm's boss, in his clearing. He strikes a ring of ground around

himself (watch for the red warning), calls his hounds when he is hurt, and fades into mist when he falls.

Silver

Silver veins glint along the roads and inside the Kesselbach mine. Mine them for Chunks of Silver, then smelt them into Silver Bars at a Furnace.

  • Deeds of Eisenwald: Novice, Adept and Master. The Master deeds give the title "of the Iron Wood".
  • Feats: Huntbreaker (defeat the Wild Huntsman 3 times) and Kobold-bane (defeat 100 Forest Kobolds).
