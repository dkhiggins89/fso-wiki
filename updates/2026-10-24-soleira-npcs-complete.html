Soleira townsfolk complete

2026-10-24

Not yet live: this arrives with the next release.

All Soleira townsfolk now have approved regional outfits in source. Father Tomas wears a black cassock with a small white collar. Duchess Leonor has a crimson gown with gold trim and a fitted gold circlet. Captain Soledad wears a gold-edged captain's hat, and Town Crier Ramon carries a brass bell.

These finish the guards, merchants, fishers, clergy and farmers introduced in Soleira's town clothes. Characters keep their dialogue, shops and Original graphics. The outfits have been checked in private worlds, talk windows and all four viewing directions.

Both regions were generated together and checked again. Nothing has been released or installed. See Winter roofs and lived-in courtyards for the approved regional buildings and props, and Locations for the current world.
