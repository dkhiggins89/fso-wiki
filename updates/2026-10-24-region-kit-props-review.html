Winter roofs and Soleira courtyards in preparation

2026-10-24

Approved 24 October: both regional sets are now integrated in source. See Winter roofs and lived-in courtyards.

Skaldmark's candidate pieces include snow-covered turf roofs, ice for ponds, drifting floes and a standalone rack of drying fish. The snowy roofs use the existing timber house walls. Ice edges and floes leave the existing water visible around them. The current sled, carved rune stones, log corners and timber pen fences already fit, so they will be reused.

For Soleira, the candidates add terracotta and blue azulejo floors, open arcades, ochre and blue striped awnings, and another wrought-iron balcony. Courtyard details include a grapevine pergola, a stork nesting on a chimney, an olive press, flowerpots arranged on steps and a guitar on a stand. The existing flowers, balcony and orange stall remain available alongside these additions.

One review sheet for each region shows the new pieces beside the existing kit. Following visual approval, a few have been placed through the region generators and checked in fresh private worlds before any release.

See Ravens, wolves and raider flags for the completed heraldry work and Locations for the current world.
