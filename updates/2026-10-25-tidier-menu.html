A tidier menu

2026-10-25

The icon row at the bottom right had grown to sixteen buttons. It now shows the six windows you open most, Bag, Equipment, Skills, Quests, Mail and Settings, and a More button (four small squares).

More opens a panel just above the row, with every other window named and grouped:

  • Social: Friends, Party and Guild.
  • Collections: Beasts, Wardrobe and Achievements.
  • Home and help: Housing, Help, and the Map editor for world builders.

Pick one and the panel closes. The More button stays lit while one of its windows is open. Press More again or Esc to close the panel. Every shortcut key works as before: F for Friends, G for Guild, N for Beasts, and so on.
