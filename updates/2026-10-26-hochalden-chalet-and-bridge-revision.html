Hochalden chalet and river bridge revision

2026-10-26

Not yet live: the flag design is approved, while the revised chalet and bridge await approval. No Hochalden art or maps are in the game.

Hochalden has its flag: the white edelweiss and gold First Star on red, with the fine gold edge from option B. The approved flag artwork is being kept unchanged.

The chalet keeps its roof pitch, wide eaves, carved balcony, flower boxes and green shutters. Its ground floor is now a full storey of stone, with timber above. Low battens cross the roof pitches, carrying rows of rounded grey stones.

The covered bridge has been redesigned as a long, low river crossing. The new view shows an open gabled portal, the deck and dark interior, a row of small side windows and masonry piers standing in the river. Its approach fits the existing stone-bridge width.

A new review sheet shows just the chalet and bridge. Approval of those revisions comes before the full building kit.

The chalet has since been approved. The bridge design is accepted, with its orientation being corrected to match the map grid. See Hochalden bridges follow the map grid for the two current versions.
