Ravens, wolves and raider flags

2026-10-24

Vindholm's dark raven spreads its wings across pale cloth. Ravnsund's wolf shows its teeth on deep red, and the Sea Wolves carry a pale wolf skull on torn black cloth at Ulfsnes.

Each approved design now appears as a pole banner, a hanging banner and a small pennant. Look for the raven in Vindholm's streets, market and boathouse; the wolf at Ravnsund's west quay and the Net Mender's House; and the skull among the tents and stockade at Ulfsnes. Small feather, fang and skull marks keep the pennants readable.

These complete the town heraldry introduced in Town banners for six towns. All nine designs now have placements prepared for Skaldmark and Soleira. See Town heraldry in preparation for the first look and Locations for the current world.
