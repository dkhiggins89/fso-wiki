Eisenwald buildings and landmarks for review

2026-10-25

Not yet live: most designs were approved and added in source. The revised castle, eave/jetty pieces and taller church were subsequently approved and added in source.

Eisenwald's building set follows the approved Hohenburg townhouse and Brauheim inn: dark oak framing, warm cream and ochre plaster, red sandstone and steep tiled roofs. The selected realm banner keeps its eagle, white star and red border.

The new landmarks are a castle on a rocky crag, the Church of the First Star, Hohenburg's town hall, a woodcarver's cottage, a brewery, a silver mine entrance and a market-square fountain.

The modular kit adds walls, shutters, jetties, gables and scalloped red roof tiles. Bread and ale stalls, a harvest maypole, a well, woodpiles, carved trade signs, a hay wagon, wine trellises and hop poles give the towns places to work and gather. The existing stone bridge pieces will be reused for crossings.

Five review sheets show the new pieces beside unchanged approved references and at smaller map sizes. Art approval comes before registration or map placement. Eisenwald's maps, inhabitants and quests will follow in later batches.

Most of this set was approved on 25 October and added in source. The revised castle, timber ends and taller church were subsequently approved and added in source. Eisenwald has no playable maps yet. Nothing has been installed or published.
