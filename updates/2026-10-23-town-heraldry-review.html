Town heraldry in preparation

2026-10-23

Updated 24 October: all nine designs are approved and placed in source. See Town banners for six towns and Ravens, wolves and raider flags.

Distinctive town banners are being prepared for Skaldmark and Soleira. Each design has a tall pole banner, a hanging wall banner and a small market pennant.

Skaldmark's designs pair Vindholm with a raven, Ravnsund with a wolf, Fjellby with an elk and Isvik with an ice star. The Sea Wolves' approved design uses a wolf skull on torn black cloth.

Soleira's designs pair Castamar with a sun over a tower, Corcova with a cork oak, Alvarena with a stork and Puerto Dorado with an anchor and sun.

The regional banners remain available. All nine approved local designs now have placements in source. See the Locations for the current world.
