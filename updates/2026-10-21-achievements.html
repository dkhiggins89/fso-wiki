Deeds, titles, the Codex and the Hall of Fame

2026-10-21

Press U (or the trophy in the bar at the bottom) to open Achievements.

Deeds

Each area has its own Deeds in three ranks: Novice, Adept and Master. When you finish a rank you are told straight away; open Achievements and choose Claim reward (keep room in your bag; the reward waits for you if it is full).

Deeds include finishing the area's quests, finding its places, defeating its monsters, taming beasts, winning beast battles (Wrenford's Master deeds need the Castle Tournament), smelting an Iron Bar and reaching levels. The window lists every deed with a tick and your progress.

Feats and titles

Feats give titles: the Slime Slayer (100 Slimes), Wolfsbane (50 Wolves), the Tamer (10 beasts tamed), the Lucky (a beast with a rare colour), Champion (win the Castle Tournament), the Collector (120 different items), the Wanderer (14 places) and the Battler (25 beast battles won).

On the Titles tab choose a title to wear. Everyone sees it after your name, for example "Rowan Hero of the Vale".

The Codex

The Codex tab shows every item in the game: the ones you have ever had in full colour, the rest as dark shapes, grouped as Gear, Materials, Food & potions and Other. Its Bestiary lists the monsters you have defeated (with counts), and it also shows the beasts you have tamed, rare colours and the places you have visited.

Hall of Fame

The Hall of Fame tab ranks every character: Overall (level), Achievements (deed rewards claimed and titles earned), Beast battles won, and every skill. Your own row is highlighted.

Deeds, feats and the Codex count from the release that brings them; earlier play is not counted.
