Eisenwald's workshops and timber streets

2026-10-25

Not yet live: these additions are in source and have not been installed or released.

The approved Eisenwald buildings and town props have been added for the future forest realm. Hohenburg's townhouse and town hall, Brauheim's inn and brewery, Tannheim's woodcarver cottage, the silver mine entrance, the First Star church and the market-square fountain share dark oak framing and warm red sandstone.

The modular kit brings cream and ochre walls, bracing, shuttered windows, sandstone bases, gables and scalloped red roof tiles. Bread and ale stalls, a well, woodpiles, a hay wagon, trade signs, wine trellises, hop poles and a harvest maypole are ready for the towns. The approved eagle-and-star banner keeps its red border, and crossings will reuse the existing stone bridge.

Mapmakers can place the complete buildings and props through their saved layouts.

The castle, dark wooden eave and jetty ends, and taller church tower were subsequently approved and added in source. Hohenburg will use the taller church. See Eisenwald castle and taller spire for the finished building kit.

Eisenwald's playable maps, inhabitants and quests follow in later batches. Nothing has been installed or published.
