White villages and red roofs - the Soleira building kit

2026-10-23

Soleira is the sun-baked realm of the south-west: white villages, courtyard cities with tiled roofs, cork oak woods and orange groves. Mappers can already build with its first pieces.

Buildings in one click

Six whole buildings to place with the map editor's Big things picker:

BuildingWhat it is
Whitewashed village houseA two-storey house and wing under barrel-tile roofs. It has grilled windows, geraniums, a bougainvillea and a green arched door.
White chapelA gable-fronted chapel with three bells in its bell gable, a stork on the roof and a tiled saint panel.
Courtyard houseA gateway with an iron gate. Behind it, a two-storey arcaded gallery runs round a tiled patio.
WindmillA La Mancha windmill with a white round tower and four lattice sails.
Castamar cathedralA sandstone front with a pointed portal and a rose window, and a tall bell tower with a blue lantern.
Fortress gateOchre rammed-earth walls and towers flying the Soleira banners, and a horseshoe arch.

You can walk behind all of them, like the other prefab buildings.

A house kit

Build your own houses cell by cell:

  • Lime-washed walls in Walls & windows, all of which join in any order:
  • plain walls, and a spot of flaked plaster;
  • grilled windows, with or without pots of geraniums;
  • shuttered windows and a balcony;
  • green or blue arched doors, and a tiled panel;
  • corners with sandstone quoins, the ochre foot band, a saw-tooth cornice under the eaves, and gable ends.
  • Barrel-tile roofs in Roofs & awnings: plain and weathered roof tiles, the eave, the ridge, the verges and a

chimney.

Trees and things for the streets

  • Trees: olive, orange, cork oak and date palm. The cork oak's trunk has been stripped for its cork.
  • Street props:
  • a prickly pear, a tiled village well, a tiled fountain and clay storage jars;
  • a tiled bench, crates of oranges and an orange stall;
  • an iron street lamp that lights up at night;
  • the Soleira banner: a golden sun with a red star on deep red.
