You are what you practise

2026-10-27

Making a character is now just a name and a hairstyle. There is no class, no stat points and no starting outfit to choose.

  • Everyone starts the same: an Adventurer in plain clothes, with a short sword, three loaves of rye bread and every stat

at 1. Elder Maren's first quests in Dawnrest give you a shield and a spare sword.

  • Stats grow from skills. Each of the five stats rises with its own skills: Strength with swords, axes, hammers,

mining, woodcutting and smithing; Stamina with defence, unarmed fighting, digging, farming, fishing and ranching; Dexterity with archery, staves, fletching, tailoring, carpentry and taming; Intelligence with the schools of magic, alchemy and appraisal; Wisdom with light and shadow magic, meditation, music, tracking and cooking. Your best skill for a stat counts most, so training one skill well moves it, and a spread of related skills helps a little.

  • Your class follows your two highest stats and changes by itself as you play. You stay an Adventurer until a

stat reaches 4, then take the first class for your highest stat (Fighter, Brawler, Ranger, Mage or Priest), and once it reaches 10 a class for your top two, such as a Swordsman, Paladin, Ninja, Bard or Wizard. A Mage who masters an element becomes a Fire, Water, Wind or Earth Mage. You get a message when your class changes.

  • Existing characters keep their skills, levels and belongings; their stats and class are worked out from their

skills the next time they log in.

  • The Skills window shows your class and stats at the top; point at a stat to see which skills raise it.
