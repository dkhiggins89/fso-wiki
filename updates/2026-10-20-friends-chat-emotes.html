Friends, better chat, emotes and speech bubbles

2026-10-20

Friends and ignore lists

Press F (or the two-people icon in the bar at the bottom) to open Friends.

  • Friends shows everyone on your list, online friends first, with where they are. Message starts a private

message to them; Remove takes them off the list. You get a notification when a friend logs in, and a line in the chat log when they log in or out.

  • Ignored lists the players you ignore. You no longer see their chat, emotes or private messages, and their trade,

party, guild and beast battle invitations do not reach you.

  • Type a name and press Add to add to the tab that is open, or right click a player for Add friend, Ignore,

Message and Report.

  • Chat commands do the same: /friend add NAME, /friend remove NAME, /ignore add NAME, /ignore remove NAME.

Your lists are saved with your character, up to 100 names each. Adding someone to one list takes them off the other.

Chat

  • A new Guild tab shows only your guild's chat (/g). The Chat tab includes it too.
  • Item links: Shift + click an item in your inventory to put [Item Name] in the chat box. Item names in square

brackets show in orange in the chat log; point at one to see what it is.

  • Settings > Interface has three new switches: speech bubbles over heads (on), the time before each chat line

(off), and hiding rude words in chat (on).

Emotes and speech bubbles

What players say now also appears in a speech bubble over their head for a few seconds. Emotes show the same way:

/wave /bow /dance /cheer /clap /laugh /cry /sit /point /think /yes /no /shrug /angry /love

The :) button beside the chat box lists them all.

Reporting a player

Right click a player and choose Report, or type /report NAME what happened. The report is kept for the staff, and any staff member who is online is told straight away. Staff can read the latest reports with /reports.
