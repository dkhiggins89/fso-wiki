A new face for your character

2026-10-29

Your character has a new face. The round white eyes are gone: each eye is now a smaller almond shape with a brown iris, a little white at the corners and a dark line along the upper lid, with skin between the two eyes. The jaw and cheeks are shaped, the nose is smaller, and the skin is a warmer golden colour. Your hands have the same golden skin in every outfit, so face and hands always match.

The new face shows under every hat, cap, crown, hood and helmet that leaves the face visible, in every direction and while walking or fighting. Where a visor leaves only a narrow slot, as on the Spangenhelm, the Huscarl Helm or the Chain Helm, you see your eyes through the slot. Helmets that close over the whole face, such as the War Helm and the Murder Helm, look as before.

Your hair is new too: instead of a smooth cap it falls in separate locks with uneven tips, fuller at the back, and it shows the same way under the crowns, the White Tiara, the Fawn Cap, the Leather Cap and the Ice Cap. The hair colour you chose when you made your character still applies, and dyed outfits work as before.

Standing still, your character now blinks every few seconds (see Characters that blink). The townsfolk have new faces and hair as well (see New faces and hair for the townsfolk). The Original graphics setting is unchanged.
