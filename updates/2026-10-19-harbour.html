The harbour, boats and a ship you can walk on

2026-10-19

For world builders

  • Stone quay (Coasts, shores & cliff edges): harbour water with a cut-stone quay wall against flagstone paving, in

every shape. Paint plain water next to flagstones with Auto edges for paths on and the quay is fitted for you.

  • Boats (Building prefabs; one click in the map editor's Buildings tab): a canoe, a fishing boat, a sailing sloop, a

cargo barge and a merchant cog. Place them on water.

  • The galleon: a three-masted ship 16 tiles long that you can walk on. Its deck is solid ground; climb aboard by the

gangplank in the middle of its near side. Place it on open water with a dock or quay just below the gangplank. Its stern lantern glows at night.

  • Dock: quay end: the end of a dock that meets a stone quay, so a dock runs from the quay out to a ship with no gap.
  • Dock crane and lighthouse (Building prefabs). The lighthouse shines at night.
  • Harbour props (Outdoor objects): gangplank, cargo pallet, sea chest, oar rack, life ring, harbour lamp (glows at

night), net over a crate, fish barrel and cannon.
