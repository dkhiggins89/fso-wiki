Steadier walking, livelier fights

2026-10-26

Walking and fighting have been reworked after a look at how the game feels to play.

Walking

  • The camera stays with you. It used to trail about a third of a tile behind while you walked and then drift on for

half a second after you stopped. Now your character stays in the middle of the screen, and the view stops the moment you do.

  • Legs that match the ground. Your character takes one stride every two tiles instead of scurrying with a stride

every tile, and the stride follows the ground actually covered, so feet no longer slide when you speed up or slow down. Other players walk the same way.

  • If you prefer the old feel, turn on Settings > Interface > Classic movement.

Monsters

  • They notice you. Hostile monsters in the wild come at you when you get within a few steps. They are slower than

you, so you can always walk away.

  • They fight back. Hit a monster, even with an arrow from across the clearing, and it comes for you.
  • They give up. A monster follows you only a short way from where it lives. Then it walks home and is back to full

health when it gets there.

  • Animals stay calm. Cows, pigs, geese, goats, reindeer, elk and the like never come for you, and neither do

things that do not move, such as webs and spikes.

  • A clearer wind-up. A monster about to land a heavy blow glows red with a ! over it. Face it and Guard, or

step away and the blow misses. The chat line explaining this now appears once, instead of filling the chat on every blow. When the blow lands, the monster lunges forward.

  • Monsters keep their walking animation going from tile to tile, and their health bar has a dark backing so it is

easier to read.

Fighting

  • How dangerous is it? Every monster is rated for you, from your own weapon, armour, stance and health against

its attack, defence and health: Trivial (grey), Easy (green), Fair (yellow), Tough (orange) or Deadly (red). The rating shows after its name when you right click it or point at it, colours the ring under it and the name in the target bar, and Examine spells it out. Better gear brings ratings down.

  • Hit splats. Damage now pops up on a small badge over whoever was struck: orange for damage you deal, red for

damage you take, blue for a blow you parried or blocked. Several blows side by side no longer pile on top of each other.

  • Critical hits. About one blow in twelve is a critical hit for more damage, shown on a bigger badge with a !.
  • Auto-retaliate. Stand still and get hit, and you fight back at once. Walking away still gets you away. Turn it

off in Settings > Interface.

  • A breather after a fight. Six seconds clear of any fight, and you recover health and spell points quickly (a

fortieth every half second, so about twenty seconds from empty to full), as long as you are not starving. The character panel shows resting while it happens.

Server owners can switch monster chasing off, or change how near a player must come and how far monsters follow, in the server app's Live events tab.
