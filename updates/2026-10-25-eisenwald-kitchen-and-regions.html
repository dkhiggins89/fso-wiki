Potatoes, hops and the Eisenwald kitchen

2026-10-25

Regions section is here now.

Three new crops

Buy seeds from Herbwife Margit in the Hohenburg gardens, plant them in a tilled plot, water them and harvest when they are ripe, as with every other crop. See Farming.

  • Seed Potatoes grow into a potato field: earth ridges with leafy plants in flower.
  • Cabbage Seeds grow into a cabbage field.
  • Hops Seeds grow into a hop garden: bines climbing strings up to a wire, hung with green cones.

Hop gardens and potato fields also grow on the farms of the Wolfbach Valley, and the Hohenburg gardens have potatoes beside their cabbages.

The Eisenwald kitchen

Cook these at a stove or campfire with Cooking:

  • Sauerkraut from cabbage and herbs.
  • Sausages from pork and herbs, two at a time.
  • Ale from barley and hops, two at a time.
  • Pretzels from rye and milk.
  • Roast Goose from a Raw Goose, potatoes and herbs. Butcher Gustav in Hohenburg sells Raw Geese.

Potatoes and cabbage are good to eat raw too.
