Skaldmark's towns grow, and its people dress for the north

2026-10-23

Vindholm, the walled city

Vindholm now covers nine maps inside a timber town wall, with gates on the east and south roads.

DistrictWhat you'll find
VindholmThe square with the Jarl's hall, the mead house and the starstone.
Vindholm HarbourThe quay and the Jarl's longship.
Vindholm MarketTorvald's smithy, four market stalls and Hakon's warehouse.
Vindholm Stave ChurchThe church and its graveyard.
Vindholm East GateThe gate tower and the road to Fjellby.
Vindholm Craftsmen's RowThe weaver, the brewer and the runecarver.
Vindholm LanesHouses with kitchen gardens.
Vindholm South GateTwo gate towers on the road from Birchdale.
Vindholm GardensHouses, cloudberry beds and rye.

Guards keep the gates, and you can go inside most of the houses.

Ravnsund, Fjellby and Isvik

  • Ravnsund has a west quay with drying racks and a salt house, and a lane of houses up the hill.
  • Fjellby has a village street to the west and high farms to the east.
  • Isvik has smokehouses to the west and more fishers' huts to the east.

Northern clothes

Every Skaldmark NPC now wears clothes of the north instead of a borrowed look. You'll see:

  • wool tunics with woven hems;
  • cloaks with fur collars and brooches;
  • apron dresses with oval brooches and strings of beads;
  • headscarves, hoods and fur hats;
  • braids and beards;
  • the guards' round helmets, mail, spears and painted shields.

New people

Vindholm's new townsfolk:

  • Weaver Astrid
  • Brewer Gerd, who sells ale and porridge
  • Runecarver Orm
  • Merchant Hakon, who sells hides and smoked salmon
  • Huntress Sigrid
  • Old Asa
  • the guards of the gates

Ravnsund, Fjellby and Isvik have new fishers, dockhands, farmwives and net makers too.

With the original graphics, these people still use the original game's characters.
