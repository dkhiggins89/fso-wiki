Eisenwald icons and bold field revisions

2026-10-25

Not yet live: the approved icons are in source only. A later pass reworked Raw Goose and the fields again and they were approved; see Potatoes, hops and the Eisenwald kitchen.

Ten approved farming and kitchen icons have been added unchanged: Hops Seeds, Hops, Cabbage Seeds, Cabbage, Seed Potatoes, Potatoes, Sausages, Pretzel, Sauerkraut and Roast Goose. Their artwork is registered in the source item library. The fields and remaining ingredient art will be reviewed before the full Farming and Cooking loops are connected.

Raw Goose has a new private preview: a pale plucked bird with a long curved neck, flat bill and two webbed feet, resting on a butcher's board. The previous pink body and trussing slot have been replaced.

The hop and potato fields have also been revised privately. They use the established dark wet-soil colours, strong black outlines and larger plant shapes. Sown hops show short posts and strings with small shoots; grown bines climb the trellis with hanging cones. Potatoes use broad rounded earth ridges, with planted tubers and shoots followed by larger leafy clumps and white flowers. The sheets show the old drafts and unchanged barley, grape and cabbage fields beside them.

Those five revised pieces remain outside the game pending approval. Cabbage field artwork, Ale and the existing silver mining and smelting chain are unchanged. No new crop codes, crop behaviour, recipes, food effects, regional generator or world pack changed in this batch.
