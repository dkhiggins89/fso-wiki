Hochalden

Hochalden

Not yet live. This region arrives with a later update. Its places, people, creatures, quests and food are listed here from the game as soon as it is in the world.

Hochalden is the planned mountain realm south of Eisenwald. Its valleys will have stone-based timber chalets, high pastures and mountain lakes, with winding paths climbing towards the glaciers.

The realm's flag design is approved: a white edelweiss with a gold First Star on red, framed by a fine gold edge. The chalet is also approved, with a full stone ground floor and roof stones resting in rows along low battens. Its broad roof, balcony, flower boxes and shutters keep their approved design.

Both covered-bridge designs are approved. One runs east–west across a north–south river; the other runs north–south with an open gabled entrance at the bottom end. Their canopies are prepared for the roof layer, with a walkable deck underneath, stone piers and approaches matching the existing stone bridge.

The full building kit is now under art review: chalet wall, roof and balcony pieces; a church, town hall, mountain inn, hay barn, dairy, pass chapel and crystal-cave entrance; village and pasture props; and rope bridges following the map grid. The new buildings use the approved chalet as their style and scale reference.

Not yet live: the first-look designs are approved, while the full building kit awaits art approval. No Hochalden art or maps have been added to the game.

The map box and room band remain provisional. Towns, routes, recipes, crops, deeds and festivals will be documented as they are built.
