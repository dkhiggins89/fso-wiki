Wrenford Vale is the heart of Astraeth and the place every new character wakes up. It feels like the English countryside: hedged lanes, thatched cottages, a stone chapel and a castle on the rise above the market.

New arrivals start in Dawnrest Hollow, where Elder Maren and Sergeant Holt teach the basics, then pass through the Whisperwood with its pond and old oaks before reaching Wrenford itself: the gate and forge, the market, Chapel Lane, the riverside cottages, the ranch and Castle Rise.

Every building has an interior, and the town's shops, bank and trainers make it the place most players come back to.

Deeds and feats

Deeds of Dawnrest

  • Novice: Finish A New Dawn, Find the Training Green, Defeat 3 Slimes. Reward: 5 × Apple, 2 × Pie.
  • Adept: Finish Sword Practice, Defeat 15 Slimes, Reach level 3. Reward: Fawn Cap, 3 × Monster Treat.
  • Master: Defeat 60 Slimes, Reach level 8, Train any skill to 15. Reward: Pig Costume; title "of Dawnrest".

Deeds of Whisperwood

  • Novice: Find Mossy Pond, Find the Old Oak Glade, Defeat a Poison Frog. Reward: 3 × Monster Treat, 5 × Apple.
  • Adept: Finish Frog Venom, Defeat 3 Wolves, Tame a wild beast. Reward: White Tiara, 2 × Beast Tonic.
  • Master: Defeat 25 Wolves, Defeat 25 Poison Frogs, Tame 5 beasts. Reward: Lizard Costume; title "the Woodwalker".

Deeds of Wrenford

  • Novice: Finish Welcome to Wrenford, Visit Chapel Lane, Climb to Castle Rise, Find Wrenford Ranch. Reward: 3 × Pie, 2 × Monster Treat.
  • Adept: Win a beast battle, Smelt an Iron Bar, Train any skill to 10. Reward: Imp Costume, 3 × Beast Tonic.
  • Master: Win the Castle Tournament, Reach level 15, Collect 60 different items. Reward: Silver Crown; title "of Wrenford".

Deeds of Wrenford Vale

  • Master: Earn the Master deeds of every area, Visit 12 places, Tame 15 beasts. Reward: Gold Crown, Cyclops Costume; title "Hero of the Vale".
