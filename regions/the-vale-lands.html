The Vale Lands

The Vale Lands
Inspired bya mix of British and northern European country
Maps101
Rooms14
Heraldrynone of its own yet

The Vale Lands surround Wrenford Vale on every side, and monsters grow tougher the further you travel from town. They are where the culture-themed realms join the old world: walk north from the Frostpeak Shrine to reach Skaldmark, south from the Drowned Chapel to reach Soleira, and east through the Stonecrest Hills to reach Eisenwald.

  • Eastmarch and Millbrook: farmland and the road east.
  • Highmoor: open moor and the standing stones of the Ninestones.
  • The Stonecrest Hills: rock, a mine with a gallery below, and the pass to Frostpeak.
  • Frostpeak: snow and the shrine on the way north.
  • The Greywood: a dark forest with the Woodcutters' Camp, Elderglade, Orc Hollow and the Greywood Ruins.
  • The Fenmire: bog and marsh with Nettle's hut and the Drowned Chapel.
  • Mirrowmere: a lake with an island, and the village of Mirrowford.
  • The Saltreach Coast and Port Saltreach: cliffs, Gull Point and the harbour, where ships sail to the other realms.
