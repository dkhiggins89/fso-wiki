Classes and stats

There is no class to choose when you make a character. Everyone starts as an Adventurer in plain clothes with Short Sword, Rye Bread x3, and all five stats start at 1.

Each stat grows from its skills: one third of the levels your best skill for it has above 1, plus a quarter as much for its other skills, up to 30. Your class follows your two highest stats: once any stat reaches 4 you take the first class of your highest stat, and once your highest reaches 10 (with your second at least 60% of it) the class for that pair. A Mage whose best school of magic is an element at level 30 or more becomes that element's Mage. Your class changes by itself as your skills grow.
